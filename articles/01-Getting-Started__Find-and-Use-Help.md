How Do I Find and Use Help?

Category: Getting Started Time: 5 minutes Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Related Topics:

Understanding the Layout
Login to PensionPro+
What This Does

Shows you how to access help resources, documentation, and support within PensionPro+. You'll never feel lost - help is always just a click away!

💡 Note: Help opens in a separate popup window, allowing you to reference help while working.

Quick Steps
Click the ? (Help) icon in the top navigation bar
Help window opens in a popup showing topics relevant to your current screen
Browse topics or search for what you need
Click a topic to view step-by-step instructions
Keep the help window open while working, or close it anytime
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Open the Help Center

Access Help:

Look in the top-right corner of your screen
Find the ? (Help) icon/button
Click it
Help window opens in a popup

💡 Tip: The help window is resizable and can be positioned anywhere on your screen for easy reference while working.

Step 2: Explore Help Topics

The Help Center organizes topics by category:

Main Categories:

📋 Getting Started - Basics and navigation
👥 Members & Records - Managing member information
💰 Contributions & Buybacks - Processing contributions
💼 Payroll & Payments - Running payroll
📊 Reports & Analytics - Generating reports
🏛️ Retirement & Benefits - Processing retirements
💵 Refunds & Distributions - Managing refunds
📈 Accounting & Compliance - Accounting tasks
📢 Communications - Letters and messages
⚙️ System Administration - System settings

How to Navigate:

Click a category to expand it
Browse topics within that category
Click a topic to view the guide

💡 Tip: The help center shows topics relevant to where you are in the system. If you're on the Members screen, member-related topics appear first!

Step 3: Search for Help

Can't find what you need by browsing?

Look for the search box at the top of the Help panel

Type keywords related to your question:

What you're trying to do ("add member")
Screen name you're on ("payroll")
Feature you're using ("upload contributions")

Results appear as you type

Click a result to view that topic

Search Tips:

Use simple keywords
Try different words if nothing matches
Search is not case-sensitive
Step 4: View Help Topics

When you click a help topic:

You'll See:

Step-by-step instructions
Clear explanations
Helpful tips and warnings
Common questions and answers
Related topics to explore

💡 Tip: Keep the help window open while working so you can refer to instructions as you go.

Step 5: Use Context-Sensitive Help

Smart Help - Shows What You Need When You Need It:

When you open help from specific screens, the system automatically:

Detects your current location (Dashboard, Member Directory, Settings, etc.)
Highlights topics related to that screen
Suggests the most relevant guide
Filters help to your current task

Example:

On the "Member Directory" screen → Help shows member-related topics
On the "Payment Center" screen → Help shows payroll/payment topics
On a "Report" screen → Help shows reporting topics

💡 Tip: Open help directly from the screen you're working on for the most relevant topics!

Common Questions

Q: Is help available offline? A: No, help requires an internet connection. However, you can print help topics from the help window for offline reference.

Q: Can I suggest new help topics? A: Yes! Contact your administrator with requests for additional documentation.

Q: How do I print a help topic? A: Open the topic in the help window, then use your browser's print function (Ctrl+P or Cmd+P) from the help window.

Q: Can I share help links with coworkers? A: Yes! Help topics can be shared. The help system is accessible to all users of PensionPro+.

Q: Is training available? A: Contact your administrator about available training sessions, webinars, or personalized training.

Troubleshooting Help Access
Issue: Help icon not visible

Solutions:

Check top-right corner of screen (Help button with ? icon)
Maximize your browser window
Clear browser cache and reload
Issue: Help window won't load

Solutions:

Check internet connection
Allow popups for PensionPro+ in your browser settings
Try a different browser
Disable popup blockers temporarily
Check corporate firewall settings with IT
Issue: Search not finding anything

Solutions:

Try different keywords
Browse categories instead
Check spelling
Use simpler, more general terms
