How Do I Add Member History?

Category: Members and Records Time: 10-15 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

Member profile must be open and accessible
You have permission to edit member records
You know which history type you need to add (Service History, Status History, Job History, etc.)

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Add a Member
View Audit History
What This Does

Allows you to add or edit multiple history types from the Member History menu. Service History is shown here as the model pattern for adding a new entry and updating an existing one. The workflow starts by locating the member, navigating to the desired history type, using the appropriate action to add, completing required fields, and saving. Existing entries are edited by clicking the record.

Allows you to record a member's employment history, service time, status changes, job information, unit assignments, deductions/earnings, and affidavit records. This history is crucial for calculating service credits, determining retirement eligibility, and maintaining accurate member records.

Quick Steps
Find and open the member's profile
Click Member History in the left navigation sidebar (or the Historical Info tab)
Navigate to the specific history type you need to add
Click the + [type] button (e.g., + Service, + Status, + Job History)
Fill in the history details
Click Save
Types of Member History

The Member History section includes the following subsections:

1. Service History

Tracks service time for pension calculations:

Begin Date and End Date
Board
Service Type
Service credits
Service Percentage
Buyback/Makeup indicators
2. Status History

Documents member status changes over time:

Status Date
Status Event (Active, Retired, Terminated, etc.)
Retirement Board
Transfer Record indicator
3. Job History

Records employment positions and compensation details:

Pay Date
Position (job title)
Department
Unit
Member Group
Full Time Status (percentage)
Annual Salary
Pay Frequency and Pay Period Count
4. Unit History

Records the units a member has belonged to:

Unit selection
Notes about participation
5. Deduction / Earns History

Tracks member deductions and earnings records:

Posting Date and Posting Number
Post Code
Board
Contribution Rate
2% Amount, Payroll Amount, Deduction Amount
Unit
Transfer Record indicator
6. Affidavit History

Manages affidavit verification records:

Verification Year
Payee information (name, SSN, birth date)
Address information
Return status and date
Comments
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Open the Member Profile
Use Global Search or Member Directory
Find the member
Click to open their profile
Step 2: Navigate to Member History

Where to Go:

Look at the navigation sidebar on the left side of member profile
Find the Member History section heading
Click to expand and see all available subsections

Subsections You'll See:

Service History - Service time records and credits
Status History - Status changes over time
Job History - Job positions and employment records
Unit History - Unit membership records
Deduction / Earns History - Deduction and earnings records
Affidavit History - Affidavit verification records

💡 Tip: You can also access Member History via the Historical Info tab in the top navigation tabs.

Step 3: Select History Type

Navigate to the appropriate subsection:

For service time and credits → Service History
For status changes → Status History
For employment positions → Job History
For unit assignments → Unit History
For deductions/earnings → Deduction / Earns History
For affidavit records → Affidavit History
Step 4: Add New History Record
Click the appropriate + [type] button in the section header:
+ Service for Service History
+ Status for Status History
+ Job History for Job History
+ Unit History for Unit History
+ Deduction / Earnings for Deduction/Earns History
A modal form opens for that history type
Fill in required fields (marked with asterisks)
Click Save
Adding Service History

Service Period:

Begin Date - When service period began
End Date - When service period ended (leave blank if ongoing)

Service Details:

Board - Select the retirement board
Service Type - Type of service being recorded
Service - Service credits amount
Service Percentage - Full-time/part-time percentage

Adjustments & Buyback:

Service Adjustment - Any adjustments to service credits
Buyback/Makeup - Indicates if this is a buyback or makeup record
Adjustment Description - Notes about adjustments

Member Information:

Member Group - The member's classification group
Open - Open record indicator
Transfer Record - Indicates if this is a transferred record
Adding Status History

Status Information:

Status Date* - Date status change became effective
Status Event* - Select from available status codes (e.g., Active, Retired, Terminated, Leave of Absence, Vested, Deceased)

Board & Transfer Information:

Retirement Board - Select the applicable board
Xfer Record - Transfer record indicator (Yes/No)
Adding Job History

Job Information:

Pay Date* - The effective date for this job record
Position - Job title/position name (e.g., "Police Officer", "Firefighter")
Department - Select from available departments

Employment Details:

Unit - The organizational unit
Member Group - Classification group
Full Time Status - Percentage (0-100, where 100 = full-time)

Compensation:

Annual Salary - Annual compensation amount
Pay Frequency - How often the member is paid
Pay Period Count - Number of pay periods
Adding Unit History

Unit Assignment:

Unit* - Select the unit the member belongs to

Additional Information:

Notes - Any notes about the member's participation in this unit

💡 Tip: Create a separate Unit History record for each unit the member has belonged to.

Adding Deduction / Earns History

Posting Information:

Posting Date - Date of the posting
Posting Number - Reference number for the posting
Post Code - Select the posting code
Board - Select the retirement board

Financial Details:

Contribution Rate - Rate of contribution
2% Amount - The 2% deduction amount
Payroll Amount - Gross payroll amount
Deduction Amount - Amount deducted

Assignment Information:

Unit - The organizational unit
Xfer Record - Transfer record indicator
Adding Affidavit History

Basic Information:

Verification Year* - The year the affidavit was generated
Payee - The payee receiving benefits
Payee SSN - Social Security Number

Name Information:

Full Name - Full name of the payee
Affidavit First Name - First name on affidavit
Affidavit Last Name - Last name on affidavit

Date Information:

Payee Birth Date - Date of birth
Date Returned - When the signed affidavit was returned

Status Information:

Returned - Whether a signed affidavit was returned (Yes/No)
Logged By - Person who entered the return information
Name Sort - Sort order

Address Information:

Address 1, 2, 3 - Street address lines
City, State, ZIP - Location details
Foreign Address - For international addresses

Comments:

Affidavit Comments - Additional notes about the verification
Step 5: Save the History Record
Review all entered information
Verify dates are correct
Click Save
✅ Confirmation message appears
New history record appears in the list
Important Considerations
Service Credit Calculations

⚠️ Service history affects:

Total years of service
Retirement eligibility
Pension benefit amounts
Vesting status

💡 Tip: Verify service dates carefully - they directly impact benefits!

Status Changes

Critical Status Changes:

Retirement:

Changes member to retired status
Activates pension payments
Stops contribution processing

Termination:

Ends active employment
May start vested rights period
Stops regular contributions

Leave of Absence:

Temporary status
May affect service credit
Member may return to active
Common Scenarios
Scenario: Adding Employment for New Hire

Steps:

Open member profile
Click Member History in the left navigation sidebar
Navigate to Job History
Click + Job History
Enter:
Pay Date: Hire date
Position: "Firefighter"
Department: Select from list
Full Time Status: 100 (for full-time)
Save
Navigate to Status History
Add "Active" status with same date
Save
Scenario: Recording a Promotion

Steps:

Open member profile
Click Member History → Job History
Find current position
Edit to add a new record with the promotion date
Save
Click + Job History for new position
Enter new job title, pay date, and salary
Save
Scenario: Member Retiring

Steps:

Open member profile
Click Member History → Status History
Click + Status
Select Status Event: "Retired"
Enter retirement effective date as Status Date
Select Retirement Board
Save
Proceed to Retire a Member workflow
Editing Existing History

To Modify a History Record:

Find the record in the history list
Click on the record (row) to open the edit window
Update fields as needed
Click Save

To Delete a History Record:

Find the record in the history list
Click on the record (row) to open the edit window
Click the Delete button (typically bottom left of the window)
Confirm deletion
Record is removed

⚠️ Warning: Deleting history can affect service calculations. Consult your administrator before deleting.

Common Questions

Q: Can I add history for dates in the past? A: Yes, you can add historical records with past dates to complete a member's record.

Q: What if I don't know the exact start date? A: Use the best available information (first of month, first of year). Add a note in comments.

Q: Can a member have multiple active job records? A: Typically no - only one current position. Close previous jobs before adding new ones.

Q: How far back should I enter history? A: Enter all history needed for accurate service credit calculations, typically back to initial employment.

Q: What if service credits don't calculate correctly? A: Verify all dates are accurate. Contact your administrator if calculations still seem wrong.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't add history record

Solutions:

Check you have edit permissions
Verify member profile is not locked
Ensure all required fields are filled
Check date formats are correct
Issue: Dates are in wrong order

Solutions:

End date must be after start date
History records should not overlap
Check year is correct (2024 vs 2023)
Issue: Service credits not calculating

Solutions:

Verify service start and end dates
Check service type is set correctly
Ensure no gaps in service periods
Contact administrator for calculation issues
Best Practices

✅ DO:

Enter history chronologically
Verify dates before saving
Add detailed comments/notes
Keep job history current
Document status changes thoroughly

❌ DON'T:

Leave gaps in employment history
Overlap history records
Delete history without consultation
Enter future dates for active records
