How Do I Add a Member?

Category: Members and Records Time: 5 minutes Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Before You Begin:

Ensure you have permission to create members
Have the member's basic information ready (SSN, DOB, name)

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Update Member Demographic Information
Add Member History
What This Does

Creates a new member record in PensionPro+ and assigns them a unique Member ID. Use this when you have a new employee joining the pension system.

Quick Steps
Click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left corner
Locate the Create section in the right-hand panel of the navigation menu
Click + Member
Enter all available and applicable information for the member
Click Save
Member profile opens with new Member ID assigned
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Open the Add Member Form

Where to Go:

Look in the top-left corner of your screen
Click the ☰ hamburger menu icon
Locate the Create section in the right-hand panel of the navigation menu
Click + Member

A form will pop up on your screen.

Step 2: Enter Member Information

On the Member Add screen, enter all available and applicable information for the member.

As a best practice:

Complete as many fields as possible at the time of creation
Accurate demographic and employment data helps ensure downstream processes such as contributions, service credit, payroll, and retirement calculations

Required Fields (marked with *):

First Name*

Enter the member's legal first name
Example: "John"

Last Name*

Enter the member's legal last name
Example: "Smith"

Social Security Number*

Format: XXX-XX-XXXX
Example: 123-45-6789
⚠️ Must be unique - system will alert you if it already exists

Date of Birth*

Click the calendar icon to select
Or type in format: MM/DD/YYYY
Example: 01/15/1980

Optional Fields:

Middle Name
Suffix (Jr., Sr., III)
Gender
Marital Status

💡 Tip: While you can add more details later, completing as much information as possible now will save time and help ensure accuracy in downstream processes.

Step 3: Save the Member
Review the information you entered
Double-check the Social Security Number
Click the blue Save button

What Happens:

✅ Green message appears: "Member created successfully"
🆔 System assigns a unique Member ID
📄 Member's profile page opens automatically
What Happens Next

The member's profile page displays with these sections on the left:

Personal Information
History
Financial
Contributions
Calculations
Documents
Reports

You're now viewing the member's complete record.

Common Questions

Q: What if the SSN already exists? A: The system will show an error message. Search for the existing member using
Find a Member
. Contact your administrator for assistance with duplicate records.

Q: Can I add a member without an SSN? A: No, SSN is required. Contact your system administrator if you have special cases.

Q: What if I make a typo? A: You can edit member information anytime using
Update Member Demographic Information
.

Q: How long does it take? A: About 5 minutes for basic information. You can add additional details later.
