How Do I Change Checking/Bank Account Details?

Category: Members and Records Time: 10 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Update Payment Information
Add Multiple Memos
What This Does

Allows you to set up or modify direct deposit information for a member. This ensures pension or payroll payments are deposited directly into the member's bank account.

Quick Steps
Find and open the member's profile
Click Financial Info → Payment Information section
Click + Payment to add a new payment record, or click an existing payment row to edit
In the modal, navigate to the Direct Deposit section
Enter bank routing and account numbers
Click Save or Save and Close
Before You Begin

Have Ready:

✅ Bank routing number (9 digits)
✅ Account number
✅ Account type (checking or savings)
✅ Copy of voided check or bank letter (recommended)

💡 Tip: Always verify bank information with the member!

Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Open the Member Profile
Use Global Search or Member Directory
Find the member
Click to open their profile
Step 2: Navigate to Bank Information

Where to Go:

Click Financial Info navigation card
Scroll to the Bank Information or Direct Deposit section
Current bank accounts display (if any)
Step 3: Add or Edit Payment Record (with Bank Account)

To Add New Payment Record:

Click + Payment button
Payment Information Add modal opens

To Edit Existing Payment Record:

Find the payment in the list
Click on the payment row to open the edit modal
Navigate to the Direct Deposit section to update bank information
Bank Account Information Fields
Required Information

Bank Name

Name of financial institution
Example: "First National Bank"

Checking Account Number*

Member's checking account number at the bank
Found on bottom of check
Can be 4-17 digits typically

Account Type*

Checking - Standard checking account
Savings - Savings account

💡 Tip: Checking accounts are most common for direct deposit.

Optional Information

⚠️ Note: The availability of these optional fields may vary. Check your system's actual form.

Account Holder Name

Name on the bank account
Should match member name

Bank Address

Bank branch address
City, State, ZIP

Effective Date

Date this account becomes active
Leave blank to activate immediately

Allocation Percentage

If splitting deposit between accounts
Must total 100% across all accounts
Step 4: Verify Information

Critical Verification Steps:

Double-check routing number (9 digits)
Verify account number carefully
Confirm account type (checking vs. savings)
Review account holder name

⚠️ Important: One wrong digit sends payment to wrong account!

💡 Best Practice: Have member provide voided check to verify information.

Step 5: Save the Account
Review all entered information
Click Save
✅ Confirmation message appears
Bank account added/updated
Validating Bank Information
Finding Routing and Account Numbers

On a Check:

┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│  JOHN DOE                           │
│  123 MAIN STREET            Check # │
│  ANYTOWN, ST 12345          001     │
│                                     │
│  PAY TO THE                         │
│  ORDER OF _________________ $______ │
│                                     │
│  __________________________________ │
│                                     │
│  First National Bank                │
│  123 Bank Street                    │
│  Anytown, ST 12345                  │
│                                     │
│  |:123456789|:  9876543210  |: 001 │
│   ^Routing      ^Account            │
└─────────────────────────────────────┘


Routing Number: 9 digits (left) Account Number: Variable length (middle)

Verifying Routing Numbers

Routing Number Check:

Must be exactly 9 digits
First two digits: 01-12 (Federal Reserve regions)
System may validate automatically

If Unsure:

Call the member's bank
Verify on bank's website
Request official bank letter
Common Scenarios
Scenario: Setting Up Direct Deposit for First Time

Steps:

Open member profile
Go to Financial → Bank Information
Click Add New
Enter:
Bank Name: "First National Bank"
Routing: 123456789
Account: 9876543210
Type: Checking
Verify all information
Save
Attach voided check or bank letter
Add memo noting setup date
Scenario: Member Changed Banks

Steps:

Open member profile
Go to Financial → Bank Information
Find old account
Either delete old account OR mark inactive
Add new bank account with new information
Verify new routing and account numbers
Save
Add memo documenting bank change
Notify payroll of change
Scenario: Member Reports Wrong Bank Information

Steps:

Verify correct information with member
Open member profile
Go to Financial → Bank Information
Edit the account
Correct routing or account number
Verify changes with member
Save
Add memo explaining correction
Alert payroll to reprocess any failed payments
Multiple Bank Accounts
Splitting Direct Deposit

⚠️ Note: The ability to split direct deposits across multiple accounts may not be available in all systems. Check with your administrator if this feature is needed.

If available, split deposits work as follows:

Example: 80% to Checking, 20% to Savings

Add first account:

Checking account
Allocation: 80%
Save

Add second account:

Savings account
Allocation: 20%
Save

Total must equal 100%

Important Considerations
Direct Deposit Activation

Timeline:

Changes may take 1-2 pay cycles to take effect
Member may receive paper check during transition
Verify with payroll team

Verification:

Confirm change before next payroll run
Member should monitor first direct deposit
Report any issues immediately
Security Best Practices

✅ DO:

Verify member identity before making changes
Request voided check or bank letter
Double-check all numbers
Keep documents confidential
Shred copies after verification

⚠️ DON'T:

Accept bank information over phone without verification
Share banking details in email
Make changes without documentation
Skip verification steps
Common Questions

Q: How long until direct deposit is active? A: Typically 1-2 pay cycles. Confirm with your payroll department.

Q: Can a member have multiple bank accounts? A: Depends on your system. Some allow splits, others allow one primary account.

Q: What if routing number is rejected? A: Verify it's exactly 9 digits and valid for the bank. Contact the bank if needed.

Q: Can family members add bank information? A: No, only the member can authorize bank account changes. Require proper authorization.

Q: What happens to pending payments when bank info changes? A: Pending payments may go to old account. Notify payroll immediately of changes.

Q: How do I know if direct deposit is working? A: Member should see deposit in account on pay date. They can verify with their bank.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Routing number won't validate

Solutions:

Verify it's exactly 9 digits
Check for typos
Confirm it's a valid US routing number
Call bank to verify
Try entering without dashes
Issue: Account number too long/short

Solutions:

Verify number from check or bank statement
Don't include check number
Don't include routing number
Contact bank for correct format
Issue: Changes not taking effect

Solutions:

Verify changes were saved
Check effective date
Confirm with payroll processing
Allow time for pay cycle
Contact payroll department
Best Practices

✅ DO:

Always verify with voided check
Double-check every digit
Test with small deposit if possible
Document all changes
Notify payroll of changes
Keep member's banking information confidential

❌ DON'T:

Rush through verification
Accept verbal information only
Make changes without proof
Share account details insecurely
Forget to notify payroll
