Overview
Members and Records

This section covers everything related to managing member information, from adding new members to updating their records and maintaining their history.

📖 Topics in This Section
Essential Member Operations
Add a Member

Create a new member record in the system. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Find a Member

Search for and locate member records using various methods. Time: 3 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Update Member Demographic Information

Modify member's basic demographic data like name, DOB, SSN. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Update Contact Information

Change member's address, phone, email, and contact preferences. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Update Payment Information

Modify member's payment details and financial information. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Member History and Records
Add Member History

Record employment history, service time, and status changes. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Update Beneficiary Designations

Add or modify member's beneficiary information. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Change Checking/Bank Account Details

Update direct deposit and banking information. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Review Contribution History

View and verify member's contribution records. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Set Up Military Service Record

Document military service for buyback eligibility. Time: 15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Working with Records
Add Multiple Memos

Create notes and documentation on member records. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

View and Manage Memos Assigned to Me

Track and complete memos that have been assigned to you for follow-up. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

View Audit History

See who changed what and when on member records. Time: 3 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Scan Documents

Digitize and attach documents to member records. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Export Member Data to Excel

Download member information for external analysis. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Advanced Navigation
Open Multiple Members in Browser Tabs

Work with several member records simultaneously. Time: 2 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Review Member Banner and Navigation Cards

Understand the member profile layout and quick access features. Time: 5 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Filter and Search Within Member Directory

Use advanced filtering to find groups of members. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Use Advanced Search

Find members who meet specific criteria using multiple filters. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate
