How Do I Review the Member Banner and Navigation Cards?

Category: Members and Records Time: 5 minutes Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Understanding Layout

What This Does

Explains the member profile layout so you understand what information appears in the banner at the top and how to navigate through different sections using the navigation cards on the left side.

Understanding the Member Banner

The member banner appears at the top of every member profile. It displays key information at a glance so you don't have to dig through multiple sections.

Banner Components
Member Name and Back Button

Top Section:

Back Arrow (←) - Returns to Member Directory
Member's Full Name - Legal name with salutation (if entered)
Example: "Mr. John Smith"
Key Statistics Bar

The banner displays these critical data points horizontally:

Status

Shows current status (Active, Retired, Terminated, etc.)
Hover to see last status update date
Click dropdown (▼) to access quick actions like "Retire Member"
Color-coded badge for visual identification:
Green: Active
Blue: Retired
Gray: Terminated
Red: Death-related statuses

Date of Birth

Format: MM/DD/YYYY
Shows age in parentheses: (45)
Updates automatically based on current date

Accumulated Deductions

Total member contributions to date
Format: $X,XXX.XX
Key indicator of retirement account balance

Year to Date Earnings

Current year's earnings
Format: $X,XXX.XX
Resets January 1st each year

Service to Date

Total creditable service years
Format: XX.XXXX years
May include purchased service and military credit
Quick Action Buttons

Details Button

Hover to see additional information:
Current Board
Current Unit
Hidden banner fields (on smaller screens)

Memo Button

View/add member memos
Badge shows number of memos
Color indicates:
Gray badge: Legacy memos only
Red badge: New memos added

Messages Button

Access member communication
Available when Messages feature is enabled

Favorite Button

Star icon to mark frequently accessed members
Click to toggle on/off
Yellow star = Favorited
Gray star = Not favorited
Navigation Cards

The navigation cards on the left organize all member information into logical sections. Each section expands to show subsections when clicked.

Available Navigation Sections
1. Demographic Data

Contains all personal information about the member.

Subsections:

Basic Info - Name, SSN, DOB, gender, marital status
Contact Info - Address, phone, email
Beneficiary Info - Primary and contingent beneficiaries
Dependent Info - Spouse and dependent information
Power of Attorney - Legal representative information
DRO Info - Domestic Relations Order information
Annuity Savings - Annuity savings account details
Paycheck Details - Paycheck distribution information
Earnings Summary - Historical earnings data
2. Member History

Tracks all historical records for the member.

Subsections:

Service History - All service periods
Status History - All status changes over time
Job History - Employment positions held
Unit History - Unit/department assignments
Deduction / Earns History - Historical deduction and earnings records
Affidavit History - Submitted affidavits
3. Military Service

Military service credit and related information.

Subsections:

Service Dates - Military service periods
Deduction Amounts - Required military service purchase amounts
Accumulated Deductions - Payments made toward military service credit
4. Financial Info

Current financial and payment information.

Subsections:

Financial Info - Bank account and routing information
Payment Info - Direct deposit settings
Check / Direct Deposit Register - Payment history
1099 Info - Tax reporting information
5. Benefit Calculations

All retirement and benefit calculations for the member.

Calculation Types Available:

Superannuation A, B & C
Option D
Termination Allowance
Ordinary Disability
Ordinary Disability - Veteran
Accidental Disability
Accidental Death Benefit
Military Purchase
Buybacks (service credit purchases)
Makeups (missed contribution periods)

Special Features:

Retirement Wizard - Step-by-step retirement processing
Buyback Payment History - Track buyback payments
Installment Calculations - Payment plan details
6. Account Disbursements

⚠️ Admin Access Required

Distribution of funds from member accounts.

Subsections:

Refund/Transfer/Rollover History - Account distribution history
Recalculate Annuity Savings - Recalculation tools
Reports & Letters - Generate distribution reports
Beneficiary Refunds - Death benefit refunds to beneficiaries
Cash Disbursements - Cash distribution records
7. Buybacks/Makeups

Service credit purchases and makeup contributions.

Subsections:

Buyback - Service credit buyback records
Makeup - Makeup contribution records
Military Purchase - Military service credit purchases
Buyback Repayment - Repayment history and status
Installment Calculation - Payment plan calculations
Makeup History By Year - Year-by-year makeup history
8. COLA

Cost of Living Adjustments for retirees.

Subsections:

COLA Increase History - Historical COLA adjustments
Pending COLA - Upcoming COLA adjustments
9. 3(8)(c)

3(8)(c) billing and compliance information.

Subsections:

Billing Percentages - 3(8)(c) billing percentage records
Yearly Billing - Annual billing records
10. Reports

Member-specific reports available for generation.

Subsections:

Various member reports
Contribution history reports
Service verification reports
Benefit estimate reports
11. Documents

All scanned and uploaded documents for the member.

Subsections:

Medical records
Beneficiary forms
Correspondence
Supporting documentation
12. Messages

Communication with the member (if Messages feature enabled).

Subsections:

Sent messages
Message history
Drafts
How to Navigate
Opening a Section
Click any section heading in the navigation cards
Section expands to show subsections
Multiple sections can be open at once
Accessing a Subsection
Click a subsection link under an expanded section
Content displays in main area on the right
Navigation cards stay visible for easy navigation
Current Location Indicator
Active section is highlighted in blue
Tells you where you currently are
Helps you track your place when working with multiple sections
Common Scenarios
Scenario 1: Reviewing a New Member

What You Need:

Basic demographics
Contact information
Employment history

Steps:

Open member profile
Review banner for key stats
Click Demographic Data → Basic Info
Click Demographic Data → Contact Info
Click Member History → Job History

⏱️ Time: 2-3 minutes

Scenario 2: Processing a Retirement

What You Need:

Service history
Benefit calculations
Payment information

Steps:

Review banner Service to Date
Click Member History → Service History (verify)
Click Benefit Calculations
Review previous calculations or run new one
Click Financial Info → Payment Info (verify bank details)

⏱️ Time: 10-15 minutes

Scenario 3: Updating Contact Information

What You Need:

Current contact info
Beneficiary information

Steps:

Click Demographic Data → Contact Info
Update address, phone, email
Click Demographic Data → Beneficiary Info
Verify beneficiaries have current contact info

⏱️ Time: 5 minutes

Scenario 4: Researching a Member Issue

What You Need:

Full history review

Steps:

Check banner Status and Service to Date
Click Member History → Status History
Click Member History → Deduction / Earns History
Click Memo button in banner
Read memos for context

⏱️ Time: 10-20 minutes

Tips for Efficient Navigation

✅ BEST PRACTICES:

Glance at banner before diving into details
Use banner stats to quickly assess member status
Keep frequently used sections expanded
Use browser tabs for comparing multiple members
Add memos as you work for future reference
Favorite frequently accessed members

💡 TIME SAVERS:

Banner provides most common info without clicking
Multiple sections can stay open simultaneously
Navigation cards persist - no need to reopen each time
Browser back button works within member profile
Right-click links to open in new tab

Common Questions

Q: Why don't I see all navigation sections? A: Some sections are hidden based on:

Your permission level
Feature flags enabled for your system
Member's status (some sections only for active/retired)

Q: Can I customize which sections appear? A: No, sections are system-defined. However, your administrator can enable/disable features that control available sections.

Q: What does the badge on the Memo button mean? A: Shows the number of memos. Gray badge = legacy memos only. Red badge = includes new memos added recently.

Q: Why is a section grayed out? A: Either:

You don't have permission to access it
The feature is disabled
It's not applicable to this member's status

Q: How do I know which section I'm currently viewing? A: The active section link is highlighted in blue in the navigation cards.

Q: Can I open multiple subsections at once? A: Yes! Multiple sections can be expanded simultaneously. Click any section heading to expand/collapse it.

Q: What's the difference between Basic Info and Contact Info? A: Basic Info = Legal name, SSN, DOB, demographics. Contact Info = Address, phone, email for communication.

Q: Why does the banner show different fields on smaller screens? A: Responsive design hides some fields on narrow screens. Click the "Details" button to see hidden information in a popup.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Banner shows incorrect data

Solutions:

Refresh the page (F5)
Check if data was recently updated (may need cache clear)
Verify in the detailed section (click into relevant area)
Report to administrator if data is truly incorrect
Issue: Navigation section won't expand

Solutions:

Click directly on the section heading text
Refresh the page
Try collapsing other sections first
Check browser console for errors
Try a different browser
Issue: Section I need is missing

Solutions:

Verify your permission level with administrator
Check if feature is enabled in your system
Confirm you're looking in the correct member profile
Some sections only appear for certain member statuses
Issue: Banner stats not updating

Solutions:

Refresh page (F5)
Navigate away and back to member
Clear browser cache
Verify data was saved in subsection
Contact administrator if issue persists

Documentation Rule

⚠️ Important for Documentation Maintainers:

When documenting navigation sections, always validate section names, ordering, and subsections against the codebase (SIDEBAR_ITEMS constant in member-container.component.ts). Do not rely on assumptions:

The correct label is "3(8)(c)", not "TEC / 3(8)(C)"
Affidavits is a subsection under Member History, not a standalone navigation section
Buybacks/Makeups is a separate navigation section with its own subsections
All navigation sections and subsections must match exactly what is implemented in the codebase
