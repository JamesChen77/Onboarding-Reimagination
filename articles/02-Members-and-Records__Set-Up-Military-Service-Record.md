How Do I Set Up a Military Service Record?

Category: Members and Records Time: 10-15 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

Member profile must be open and accessible
Military service dates must be documented and verified
Deduction information should be collected and ready
Confirm member's begin and end dates of military service

Required Documentation:

✅ DD-214 (Certificate of Release or Discharge from Active Duty)
✅ Service dates (start and end)
✅ Branch of service
✅ Type of service (Active Duty, Reserve, Guard)
✅ Discharge type (Honorable, General, etc.)

💡 Tip: Always request official military documentation before entering service records.

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Add Member History
Scan Documents
What This Does

Guides you through recording military service information for a member's pension record. This process captures military service dates and associated deduction amounts, which affects how service time is calculated for retirement benefits and pension eligibility.

Allows you to document a member's military service, which may be eligible for purchase as service credit toward their pension. This is important for members who served in the military and want to buy back that time for retirement purposes.

Quick Steps
Find and open the member's profile
Click Military Service navigation card in the sidebar menu
Click Add Military Service or + Military Service button
Enter service branch, dates, and type
Enter begin and end dates of military service
Input deduction information (civilian deduction amounts)
Attach DD-214 or service documentation
Click Save to confirm and save the new military service entry
Calculate buyback cost if member wants to purchase

Use Global Search or Member Directory
Find the member

Where to Go:

Look at the navigation cards on the left side
Click Military Service
Military records section displays
Step 3: Add Military Service Record
Click + Service Dates button
Military service form opens
Fill in required information

Service Credit Calculation

⚠️ Note: Service credit calculations may be handled through a separate buyback process rather than within the Military Service card. See the "Calculating Military Buyback" section below for more information.

Total Service Time:

System may calculate automatically
Based on start and end dates
Displayed in years, months, days

Eligible Service Credit:

Amount eligible for pension credit purchase
May exclude certain periods
Depends on service type and discharge

Service Credit Cost:

Amount to purchase service time
Calculated based on salary and actuarial tables
May include interest

Eligibility Requirements

Typical Requirements:

Honorable discharge
Did not receive military pension for same service
Service was before public employment (usually)
Member is active in pension system

⚠️ Important: Eligibility rules vary by system. Consult your plan documents.

Multiple Service Periods

If Member Served Multiple Times:

Add each period separately
Each gets own record with dates
Can calculate combined cost
Track each period independently

Example:

Period 1: 1990-1994 (Active Duty)
Period 2: 2001-2005 (Reserves)
Common Scenarios
Scenario: Member Provides DD-214 for First Time

Steps:

Review DD-214 for accuracy
Open member profile
Go to Military section
Click + Service Dates
Enter service information:
Branch (from DD-214)
Start Date (from DD-214)
End Date (from DD-214)
Discharge Type (from DD-214)
Save the service dates
Navigate to Documents section and upload DD-214 (categorize as "Military Records")
Offer to calculate buyback cost if interested
Scenario: Calculating Military Buyback Cost

Steps:

Ensure military service record exists
Verify member's current salary is up to date
Click Calculate Military Buyback
Review calculation results
Print or save calculation for member
If member wants to proceed, initiate buyback process
Scenario: Correcting Military Service Dates

Steps:

Request corrected documentation
Open member profile
Go to Military section
Find service record
Click Edit
Update dates
Add memo explaining correction
Save
Recalculate buyback if applicable
Verification Best Practices

✅ Always Verify:

Dates match DD-214 exactly
Branch of service is correct
Discharge type is accurate
Documentation is authentic

📋 Documentation Checklist:

DD-214 obtained and scanned
Dates verified against official records
Discharge type confirmed
Member informed of buyback options
Calculations performed if requested
All documents attached to record
Common Questions

Q: What if member doesn't have DD-214? A: Member can request from National Archives or their service branch. Provide guidance on obtaining records.

Q: Can reserves or guard service be purchased? A: Depends on your plan rules. Often yes, but may have different calculations.

Q: What if member received military pension? A: May not be eligible to purchase. Check your plan's rules about dual pensions.

Q: How long does member have to purchase military service? A: Varies by plan. Some require purchase before retirement, others allow after.

Q: Can family members provide military records? A: Only with proper authorization. Member must authorize release of information.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't calculate buyback cost

Solutions:

Verify military service record is complete
Check member has current salary on file
Ensure service dates don't overlap with employment
Contact administrator for calculation errors
Issue: Discharge type not available in dropdown

Solutions:

Select closest match
Add clarification in notes
Contact administrator to add option
Issue: Service dates overlap with employment

Solutions:

Verify dates are correct
Check if member was on military leave from employment
Adjust service dates if error
Document situation in memo
Best Practices

✅ DO:

Always request official DD-214
Verify all information before entering
Attach all military documentation
Calculate buyback cost for member
Document everything clearly
Follow plan rules for eligibility

❌ DON'T:

Accept unofficial documents
Enter data without verification
Skip documentation attachment
Make assumptions about eligibility
Promise buyback approval
