How Do I Update Payment Information?

Category: Members and Records Time: 10 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Related Topics:

Find a Member
Change Checking/Bank Account Details
Add Financial Information
What This Does

Allows you to update a member's payment-related information including payment type, payee, direct deposit details, payment amounts, deductions, and tax withholding settings.

Quick Steps
Find and open the member's profile
Click Financial Info in the left navigation
Scroll down to Payment Information or click Payment Info in the left navigation
Click on an existing payment row to edit, or click + Payment to add new
Update payment information fields in the modal
Click Save or Save and Close
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Open the Member Profile
Use Global Search or Member Directory
Find the member
Click to open their profile
Step 2: Navigate to Financial Info Section

Where to Go:

Look at the left-side navigation panel
Click to expand the Financial Info section
You'll see two subsections: Financial Info and Payment Info

What You'll See in the Financial Info Subsection:

The Financial Information subsection displays read-only financial data including:

Annuity information (Annuity Savings, Annuity Interest, Total Annuity Reserve, Total Annuity Paid, Calculated Annuity Balance)
General information (Pre 1988 / Non-taxable, Tax Offset, Workers Compensation amounts)
Salary information (Equivalent Salary, Average Salary, Option C Popup Factor)
Original benefits (Original Pension, Original Veterans Allowance, Original Annuity, Original Dependent Allowance, Original Retirement Allowance)
Current benefits (Current Monthly Retirement Allowance, Current Annual Retirement Allowance)
COLA information (Pre 1981 COLA, 1981-1997 COLA, Post 1997 COLA, Section 90 Amount, Total Life to Date COLA)

⚠️ Note: The Financial Information subsection is NOT where you edit payment information. To edit payment data, you must navigate to the Payment Information subsection.

Step 3: Edit Payment Information

Navigate to Payment Information:

Either scroll down to the Payment Information subsection, OR
Click Payment Info in the left-side Financial Info navigation

What You'll See:

A table displaying existing payment records with columns:

Payment Type
Payee
Annuity
Pension
Net Payment
Unit

To Edit or Add:

To edit an existing payment: Click on the payment row you want to edit
To add a new payment: Click the + Payment button in the top-right header

The Payment Information Edit modal opens with four tabs:

Tab 1: Retirement Information

Section	Fields
Calculation Details	Calculation Selection, As Of Date, Unit
Payroll Settings	Pay Status, Print Notice of Deposit, Lock Date, This Payment Only, 90 B, One Time Payment

Tab 2: Payment Information

Section	Fields
Basic Payment Information	Payment Type, Payee
Direct Deposit Group 1	ABA Number, Bank Name, Account Type, Checking Account Number, Confirm Checking Account Number, Payment Amount
Direct Deposit Group 2	ABA Number, Bank Name, Account Type, Checking Account Number, Confirm Checking Account Number, Payment Amount

Tab 3: Amounts and Deductions

Section	Fields
Payment Amounts	Annuity, Pension, Pre 81 COLA, Post 81 COLA, Post 97 COLA, Dependent Allowance, Add Annuity, Vet Allowance, Chapter 389 Amount, Gross Payment, Net Payment
Tax Deductions	Federal Tax, State Tax
Other Deductions	Deduction 1-8 (Amount and Type), DOR Case Number
Additional Information	Print Check/Notice of Deposit, Comments

Tab 4: Taxes

Section	Fields
Federal - Original	Calc Fed Tax, Fed Exemptions, Add Fed W/H
Federal - Revised	Withholding, Marital Status, 2biii: Income from Job/Pensions/Annuities, 3: Claim Dependents, 4a: Additional Income, 4b: Additional Deductions, 4c: Additional Withholding
Step 4: Save Changes
Review all updates across the relevant tabs
Click Save to save and continue editing, OR
Click Save and Close to save and return to the Payment Information list
✅ Confirmation message appears
Payment information updated
Important Considerations

⚠️ Changes May Affect:

Future payment calculations
Pension benefit amounts
Payroll deductions
Tax withholdings
Direct deposit routing

💡 Tip: Verify with payroll team before making significant changes.

Common Scenarios
Scenario: Updating Direct Deposit Information

Steps:

Open member profile
Navigate to Financial Info → Payment Info
Click on the payment row to edit
Go to Payment Information tab
Update ABA Number, Bank Name, or Account Number in Direct Deposit Group 1 or 2
Confirm the account number when prompted
Save and Close
Scenario: Adjusting Payment Amounts

Steps:

Open member profile
Navigate to Financial Info → Payment Info
Click on the payment row to edit
Go to Amounts and Deductions tab
Update Annuity, Pension, or other payment amounts
Verify Gross Payment and Net Payment calculations
Save and Close
Scenario: Adding a New Deduction

Steps:

Open member profile
Navigate to Financial Info → Payment Info
Click on the payment row to edit
Go to Amounts and Deductions tab
Click Add Deduction Amount Row if needed
Enter the Deduction Amount and select Deduction Type
Save and Close
Scenario: Updating Tax Withholding

Steps:

Open member profile
Navigate to Financial Info → Payment Info
Click on the payment row to edit
Go to Taxes tab
Update Federal - Original or Federal - Revised settings as needed
Save and Close
Validation Rules

System Checks:

Account numbers must match in confirmation fields
Payment Type and Payee are required fields
Direct Deposit fields require valid ABA/routing numbers
Deduction amounts must be valid numbers
Common Questions

Q: What's the difference between Financial Information and Payment Information? A: Financial Information displays read-only historical data about annuities, salaries, and benefits. Payment Information is where you manage active payment records including direct deposit, deductions, and tax settings.

Q: How do I split direct deposits between two accounts? A: Use Direct Deposit Group 1 and Group 2 in the Payment Information tab. Enter the bank details for each account and specify the Payment Amount for each group.

Q: Can I add multiple deductions? A: Yes, you can add up to 8 deduction rows using the "Add Deduction Amount Row" button on the Amounts and Deductions tab.

Q: What happens when I change Pay Status? A: Pay Status determines whether a payment is issued during payroll. Changing this affects whether the member receives their payment.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't find the Edit button

Solution:

Payment Information doesn't use a traditional Edit button. Instead:

Click directly on a payment row to open the edit modal
Or click + Payment to add a new payment record
Issue: Account numbers don't match error

Solution:

Re-enter the account number in both the Account Number and Confirm Account Number fields
Ensure they match exactly
Try again
Issue: Changes don't appear in payroll

Solutions:

Verify changes were saved (check for confirmation message)
Check Pay Status - ensure it's set to allow payments
Check effective dates and lock dates
Confirm payroll has been run after changes
Contact payroll team to verify
Best Practices

✅ DO:

Verify direct deposit information before updating
Use both Direct Deposit Groups when splitting payments
Document changes in the Comments field
Double-check ABA/routing numbers against bank documentation
Notify affected parties of changes

❌ DON'T:

Make changes without verification
Forget to confirm account numbers
Skip reviewing the Net Payment calculation
Leave required fields empty
