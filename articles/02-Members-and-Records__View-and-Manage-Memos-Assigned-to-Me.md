How Do I View and Manage Memos Assigned to Me?

Category: Members and Records Time: 5-10 minutes Difficulty: ⭐ Beginner

Related Topics:

Add Multiple Memos
Find a Member
View Audit History
Before You Begin
You have access to PensionPro+ with proper user permissions
At least one memo has been assigned to you
Your user account is active and linked to the system
What This Does

Explains how to locate, view, and manage memos assigned to you in PensionPro+. You'll learn how to filter memos, review memo details, update actions and due dates, and mark memos as acknowledged to track task completion and prevent duplicate work.

Quick Steps
Access My Memos Section - Navigate to the My Memos section on the PensionPro+ homepage
Filter Your Memos - Use available filters (All, 2W, M, 2M, Overdue, Show Acknowledged) to view specific memo categories
Open a Memo - Click on a memo to view full details
Update Memo Information - Edit text, due dates, assigned users, or acknowledgment status
Save Changes - Save any updates to the memo
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Access Your Assigned Memos

Where to Find Them:

Option 1: Dashboard Widget

From your Dashboard, look for the Memos or My Tasks widget
Shows count of pending memos assigned to you
Click to see full list

Option 2: Navigation Menu

Click the hamburger menu (☰) in the top-left corner
Look for My Tasks, Assigned Memos, or similar section
Click to open your memo list
Step 2: Review Your Assigned Memos

What You'll See:

The memo list displays:

Date - When the memo was assigned
Member Name - Which member the memo relates to
Subject - Brief description of the memo
Assigned By - Who assigned the memo to you
Due Date - When action is expected (if set)
Priority - Normal, Important, or Urgent

Sorting and Filtering:

Sort by date, priority, or due date
Filter by status (pending, completed, overdue)
Search by member name or memo content
Step 3: View Memo Details
Click on a memo to open it
Read the full memo content
Review any attached documents or notes
Note the requested action

Memo Detail View Shows:

Complete memo text
Original author and date
Member information
Related documents (if any)
History of actions taken
Step 4: Take Action on a Memo

Options for Handling Memos:

Open Member Record:

Click the member name to go directly to their profile
Complete the requested task
Return to memo to mark complete

Add Response:

Click Add Response or Add Note
Document what action you took
Include relevant details

Mark as Complete:

Click Complete or Mark Done
Optionally add completion notes
Memo moves to completed list

Reassign to Another User:

Click Reassign if available
Select another user
Add reason for reassignment
Memo transfers to new assignee

Set Reminder:

Click Remind Me or similar option
Set date/time for reminder
Receive notification at that time
Managing Your Memo Workload
Prioritizing Memos

Focus on:

Urgent memos first (marked with !)
Overdue memos (past due date)
Important memos
Normal priority memos

Time Management Tips:

Set aside specific times to review assigned memos
Complete quick tasks immediately
Schedule time for complex tasks
Don't let memos pile up
Filtering Your Memo List

Filter Options:

Filter	What It Shows
All	Every memo assigned to you
Pending	Memos awaiting action
Completed	Memos you've finished
Overdue	Memos past their due date
High Priority	Important and urgent memos only
Common Scenarios
Scenario: Responding to a Benefit Question

Steps:

Open the assigned memo
Note the member's question
Click member name to open their profile
Research the answer
Contact member (phone/email)
Return to memo
Add response documenting your action
Mark as complete
Scenario: Following Up on Missing Document

Steps:

Open the assigned memo
Note which document is needed
Check member's Documents section
If still missing, contact member
Add note about your follow-up attempt
If received, attach document and mark complete
If not received, add note and set reminder
Scenario: Reassigning a Memo

Steps:

Open the assigned memo
Determine why you can't handle it
Click Reassign
Select appropriate team member
Add reason: "Reassigning to [Name] who handles [Type] requests"
Submit reassignment
Common Questions

Q: How do I get memos assigned to me? A: Other staff members can assign memos to you when they create or edit a memo and select you as the assignee.

Q: Can I see completed memos? A: Yes, use the filter to show "Completed" memos to review your history.

Q: What if I can't complete a memo? A: You can reassign it to another user, or add a note explaining the delay and set a reminder.

Q: Do I get notified of new assigned memos? A: Yes, you should receive notifications for new assignments. Check your notification settings.

Q: Can I assign memos to myself? A: Yes, you can create reminders for yourself by assigning memos to your own account.

Troubleshooting
Issue: No memos showing

Solutions:

Check your filter settings (might be filtering out memos)
Verify you're logged in with correct account
Refresh the page
Check if others can see your assignments
Issue: Can't mark memo as complete

Solutions:

Ensure you have permission to complete memos
Check if required response field needs input
Try adding a completion note first
Contact your administrator if issue persists
Best Practices

✅ DO:

Check assigned memos daily
Respond promptly to urgent items
Add clear notes about actions taken
Mark items complete when done
Reassign if you can't handle something

❌ DON'T:

Ignore assigned memos
Leave memos pending indefinitely
Mark complete without taking action
Reassign without reason
