How Do I Process a Buyback?

Category: Contributions and Buybacks Time: 15-25 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Expert

Before You Begin:

Verify member's eligibility for buyback
Member is eligible for buyback (previously refunded service or new service credit purchase)
Have documentation of prior service (DD-214, employment records, etc.)
Refund date and amount from prior system/board available (if repurchasing refunded service)
Member authorization obtained for buyback
All service history documentation on file
Understand your plan's buyback rules and limits
Know actuarial or fixed cost method used
Board or administrative approval secured (if required)
Have authority to process buybacks

What This Does

Walks you through the complete buyback process in PensionPro+. A buyback covers service where the member was not yet a member of the system — such as prior refunded service, contract work, military service, or service in a different retirement system. It represents purchasing service credit the member never contributed toward. The system calculates the repayment amount based on the original refund amount, the buyback date, service period, and selected interest basis, then processes the transaction.

This increases the member's total service time, potentially affecting retirement eligibility and benefit amounts.

Key distinction from make-ups: A make-up covers service where the member was already a member of the system but contributions were not deducted from their compensation — such as during unpaid leave or due to a missed payroll deduction — and represents catching up on contributions they should have made. A buyback, by contrast, covers service where the member was not yet in the system, and represents purchasing service credit they never contributed toward.

Types of Buybacks
Military Service Buyback

What it covers:

Active duty military service
Reserve or National Guard service
Service academy time
Qualifying military periods

Requirements:

DD-214 or equivalent documentation
Service must meet plan criteria
Member must be active employee
May have time limits (e.g., within 5 years of hire)

Cost basis:

Actuarial cost method (most common)
Fixed percentage of salary
Plan-specific formula
Prior Public Employment

What it covers:

Service with another government employer
Previous service with same employer (after break)
Transferred service from another system
Part-time or seasonal prior service

Requirements:

Employment verification from prior employer
Dates of service
May require no refund was taken
Reciprocity agreement may apply

Cost basis:

Actuarial calculation
Transfer of funds from other system
May include actuarial increase for age
Private Sector Employment

What it covers:

Work for private employer before public employment
Contract work that should have been covered
Misclassified employment periods

Requirements:

Usually more restrictive
May require special authorization
Documentation of employment
Plan must allow private service purchase

Cost basis:

Usually full actuarial cost
Higher cost than public service
May be limited by IRS rules
Leave Without Pay (Extended)

What it covers:

Extended unpaid leave periods
Sabbatical time
Personal leave
Time between termination and rehire

Requirements:

Must be approved leave
May have time limits for purchase
Member must return to service
Documentation of leave period

Cost basis:

Similar to make-ups (contributions + interest)
OR actuarial cost depending on plan
Understanding Buyback vs. Make-Up
Aspect	Buyback	Make-Up
Member Status	Member was not yet in the system during the period	Member was already in the system during the period
What It Covers	Prior refunded service, military, contract work, service in a different system	Periods where contributions were not deducted from compensation (leave, missed deductions)
Nature	Purchasing service credit the member never contributed toward	Catching up on contributions the member should have made
Calculation	Refund repayment + interest (Regular, Actuarial, or Correction Interest method)	Total Deductions + Total 2% + Total Interest = Makeup Amount
Complexity	High	Moderate
Documentation	Extensive (DD-214, employment records, refund records)	Payroll and earnings records
Cost	Typically higher (refund repayment + accumulated interest)	Typically lower (contributions + interest)
Approval	Often requires board approval	Usually administrative

Step 4: Calculate Buyback Cost

To access the Buyback calculation function:

Open member's profile
Navigate to Buybacks/Makeups section
Click Buyback tab
Click + Buyback button
Buyback – Calculation Step popup opens

This is the required entry point for all buyback calculations, regardless of calculation method.

Buyback – Calculation Step Fields

The popup contains the following input fields:

Required Fields:

Buyback Type: Select the calculation method (see below)
Refund Service Begin Date: Beginning date for the period of service covered by the refund
Refund Date: The member's refund date
Refund Service End Date: Ending date for the period of service covered by the refund
Refund Amount: The amount of the refund that the member is buying back
Refund Board: The board that the service was accrued at
Buyback Date: Date of the buyback

Optional Fields (depending on Buyback Type):

Service Years: Number of years of service (system calculates if blank)
Investment Return: Required for Actuarial Interest type only
Service Months: Number of months of service (system calculates if blank)
Full or Partial Interest: Required for Actuarial Interest type only
Buyback Types (Calculation Methods)

There are three buyback types available in PP+:

Method A: Regular Interest

Uses PERAC-determined regular interest rates
Interest rates are predefined by the system
Most straightforward calculation method
System automatically calculates using regular interest rate table

Method B: Actuarial Interest

Uses investment return assumptions to calculate interest
Requires selection of Investment Return Assumption percentage
Requires selection of Full or Partial Interest option
Interest rates are determined by the selected Investment Return Assumption
More complex calculation for actuarial scenarios

Method C: Correction Interest

Uses correction interest rates for error corrections
Special calculation method for correcting previous errors
Interest rates follow correction interest rate table
Used when buyback is the result of a correction or adjustment

How calculations work:

Enter all required fields in the Buyback – Calculation Step popup
Select appropriate Buyback Type
If Actuarial Interest: Also select Investment Return and Full/Partial Interest
Click Calculate button
System performs calculation and displays results:
Year of Refund results (amount refunded, interest factor, partial year interest, end of year balance)
Year of Refund + 1 to Buyback Year - 1 results (cumulative interest factor, balance)
Buyback Year results (interest factor, interest, repayment amount)
Review calculated repayment amount
Click Save to save the calculation
Click Post when ready to finalize (updates Buyback History, Annuity Savings, and Status & Service History)

💡 Note: "No Interest" is not available as a buyback type option.

Scenario 1: Military Buyback - Standard Process

Situation: Member wants to buy back 3 years active duty military service

Member: John Smith, hired 2020, wants to buy military service 2015-2018

Process:

Member inquires about military buyback
Verify eligibility: ✓ Active employee, within time limit
Request DD-214
Member provides DD-214 showing:
Service: 01/05/2015 - 01/04/2018
Honorable discharge
Active duty (qualifies)
Set up prior service record in system
Send to actuary for cost calculation
Actuary returns: $18,500 cost
Generate quote for member
Provide quote: $18,500 for 3.00 years credit
Member accepts
Member chooses payroll deduction: $385/month × 48 months
Set up payroll deduction
Track payments over 4 years
Final payment received: June 2028
Post 3.00 years service credit
Update member record: Now has 11 years total (8 current + 3 military)
Send completion certificate

Result: Member successfully purchased military time, retirement eligible 3 years earlier

⏱️ Time: Initial setup 45 minutes; tracking over 4 years; completion 20 minutes

Scenario 2: Prior Employer - Quick Lump Sum

Situation: Member worked 5 years for neighboring county, wants to buy back

Member: Jane Doe, hired 2022, prior service 2015-2020

Process:

Member requests buyback
Verify eligibility: ✓ Eligible
Request employment verification from prior employer
Verification received, confirms: 01/01/2015 - 12/31/2019 (5 years)
Set up prior service record
System calculates using formula: 5 years × 10% × $72,000 = $36,000
Generate quote: $36,000
Provide quote to member
Member accepts immediately
Member pays lump sum via check: $36,000
Check received, deposited, cleared
Post 5.00 years service credit immediately
Update member record: Now has 7 years total (2 current + 5 prior)
Send completion certificate

Result: Buyback completed in 3 weeks, member gained 5 years credit

⏱️ Time: 60 minutes total administrative time

Common Questions

Q: How much does a buyback cost? A: Varies significantly based on years purchased, member's age and salary, and calculation method. Military buybacks typically cost 5-15% of current salary per year purchased. Prior employment actuarial costs are usually higher. Get a quote for specific cost.

Q: How long does the buyback process take? A: From start to finish: 1-2 weeks for setup and calculation using PP+, plus payment time. Lump sum: complete in weeks. Installments: months to years depending on payment plan (tracked outside PP+). External actuarial review may add 1-3 weeks if required by your plan.

Q: Can a member buy back partial service? A: Usually yes. Member can choose to buy all or part of qualifying service. Cost is prorated based on time purchased.

Q: What happens if member terminates employment during payment plan? A: Depends on plan rules. May require immediate payoff, may allow continued payments, or may forfeit service credit and refund contributions. Check plan document.

Q: Can member use retirement account money to pay buyback? A: Sometimes. If plan allows, member may roll over funds from 401(k) or other qualified plan to pay for buyback. Must follow IRS rules. Member should consult tax professional.

Q: Does bought-back service count toward retirement eligibility? A: Usually yes. Purchased service typically counts toward both eligibility requirements (years needed to retire) and benefit calculations. Verify with your plan rules.

Q: Is buyback cost tax-deductible? A: Tax question - member should consult tax professional. May depend on pre-tax vs. post-tax treatment and individual circumstances.

Q: Can member finance buyback through a loan? A: Member can obtain personal loan if plan doesn't offer installments. Plan itself typically doesn't provide loans, but may offer installment payment arrangements.

Q: What if documentation is lost or unavailable? A: For military: Can request DD-214 copy from National Archives. For prior employment: Try HR department, payroll records, W-2s, social security statements. May use alternative documentation with approval.

Q: Can a retiree buy back service? A: Usually no. Most plans require active employment. Check your plan rules for exceptions.

Q: How does buyback affect monthly retirement benefit? A: Increases benefit based on additional service years and plan formula. System administrator can provide estimate. Each year typically increases benefit by 1-3% of salary depending on plan formula.

Q: Can member buy back service if they took a refund previously? A: Sometimes. Member usually must repay the refund amount plus interest as part of buyback cost. Some plans don't allow refunded service buyback. Check plan rules.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Documentation insufficient or incomplete

Solutions:

Contact source organization for proper documents
Request specific items needed
Check if alternative documentation acceptable
National Archives for military (DD-214)
Social Security Administration for earnings verification
May need member's authorization to release information
Get supervisor approval for alternative documentation
Issue: Actuarial cost seems very high

Solutions:

Verify all information sent to actuary is correct (age, salary, service period)
Request breakdown of calculation
Compare to similar buybacks in your system
Check if quote is per-year vs. total cost
Have supervisor review
If still concerning, get second actuarial opinion
Explain to member why cost is high (age, years, etc.)
Issue: Member can't afford lump sum, but plan doesn't allow installments

Solutions:

Check if payroll deduction available (different from installments)
Member may obtain personal loan
Member may delay buyback until can afford
Member may buy partial service (fewer years)
Check for any hardship provisions
Verify plan rules are current (may have changed)
Consider board policy exception request
Issue: Prior employer won't provide verification

Solutions:

Try different contact (HR vs. payroll vs. former supervisor)
Request in writing on official letterhead
Offer to pay verification fee if required
Check if state/county records available
Use W-2s as partial documentation
Social Security earnings statement
Union records if applicable
May need subpoena (extreme cases)
Issue: Payment tracking system not working

Solutions:

Set up manual tracking spreadsheet
Create payment tracking form
Use calendar system for reminders
Contact IT for system fix
Document all payments carefully
Keep copies of checks/receipts
Reconcile regularly with accounting
Consider email/letter confirmations for each payment
Issue: Service credit not posting after payment complete

Solutions:

Verify payment actually fully received and cleared
Check system for processing errors
Verify buyback record status updated
Try manually posting service credit
Check for system permissions issues
Review audit log for errors
Contact system administrator
May need to reverse and re-post
Issue: Member has service from multiple employers/periods

Solutions:

Create separate buyback record for each period
Calculate cost for each separately
Allow member to choose which to buy
May bundle into single payment if all purchased
Track each period's status independently
Document clearly which purchased and which not
Update service history accurately
