Contributions and Buybacks

This section covers all aspects of contribution processing, buyback calculations, and make-up payments.

📖 Topics in This Section
Upload Contributions

Upload contribution files from employers into the system. Time: 15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Create Manual Contribution Register

Manually enter contributions when file upload isn't available. Time: 20 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Calculate Make-Ups

Calculate make-up payments for missed contributions with interest. Time: 15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Process a Buyback

Process member buyback of prior service (military, other employment). Time: 25 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Expert
