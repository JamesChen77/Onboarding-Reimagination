Retirement and Benefits

This section covers processing member retirements, running benefit calculations, and managing retiree information.

📖 Topics in This Section
Retire a Member

Complete retirement wizard workflow from status change through payment setup. Time: 30-45 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Expert

Run a Pension Calculation

Calculate pension benefits for various retirement scenarios. Time: 20 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Review Calculation Inputs and Outputs

Understand and verify calculation details. Time: 15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Review Retiree Financial Information

View and manage retiree payment and deduction information. Time: 10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Run, Save, and Share Benefit Calculation Results

Export calculations to PDF/Word, save to Document Center, and share with members. Time: 10-15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

View or Resume Unfinished Retirement Wizard

Find and continue in-progress retirement wizards that were paused. Time: 5-10 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Process Retiree Affidavits

Collect and track annual affidavits from retirees for compliance. Time: 15 minutes | Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

🎯 Common Workflows
Processing a Retirement

Step 1: Calculate Benefits (Estimation Phase)

Run calculations to estimate benefit amounts
Discuss calculation results and options with member
Member reviews and selects preferred benefit option
Export and share calculation with member

Step 2: Execute Retirement (Changes Member Status) 5.
Retire the member
through Retirement Wizard

Access via Status dropdown → Retire Member (NOT from Benefit Calculations)
Complete wizard steps: status change, benefit election, payment setup
Finalize retirement

💡 Key Point: Benefit Calculations (step 1) are for estimation only and do NOT change member status. The Retirement Wizard (step 2) is what actually retires the member.
