How Do I Retire a Member?

Category: Retirement and Benefits Time: 30-45 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Expert

Before You Begin:

Member must be eligible for retirement (age and service requirements)
All service history should be complete and verified
Final salary information must be current
Member has selected benefit option
Bank information is available (for direct deposit)
All required approvals obtained (if applicable)

Related Topics:

Run Pension Calculation
Add Member History
Update Payment Information
View/Resume Unfinished Retirement Wizard
What This Does

Walks you through the complete retirement process using a guided 6-step wizard that automatically saves your progress. The Retirement Wizard helps you change a member's status to retired, calculate their benefits, set up payments, and finalize their retirement. This is the most comprehensive process in PensionPro+ and handles everything from status change through first payment setup.

Quick Steps Overview
Select Active Member - Find the member in the member list with "Active Member/Enrolled" status
Create Retirement Status - Change member to "Retired" (or skip if already done)
Select & Run Calculation - Choose calculation type and run benefit calculation
Review Calculation Results - Verify and finalize calculation details
Confirm Retirement Details - Review all retirement information
Set Up Payment Information - Enter payment amounts, deductions, and bank details
Complete & Finalize - Final review and complete the retirement 🎉
Understanding the Retirement Wizard

The Retirement Wizard is Your Guided Assistant:

Think of it as a checklist that walks you through each step of retirement, ensures you don't miss anything, and saves your progress automatically so you can pause and resume anytime.

Key Features:

The 6 Steps Explained
Step 1: Create Retirement Status ⭐

Purpose: Change member's status from "Active" to "Retired" in the system.

What Happens:

Member status is updated
Retirement date is recorded
Triggers retirement processes
Makes member eligible for retiree benefits

If Already Retired: System detects this and may skip this step.

Time: 5 minutes

Step 2: Select & Run Calculation ⭐⭐⭐

Purpose: Calculate the member's monthly benefit amount.

What Happens:

Choose the applicable calculation type for this member
Enter/verify calculation parameters
System calculates benefit
Results are saved to wizard

Calculation Types:

Each board has different calculation types configured for their plan. The options displayed in the wizard will vary depending on your board's setup. Common examples include Superannuation, Accidental Disability, Ordinary Disability, Termination Allowance, and Accidental Death — but the exact types available to you will depend on your board's configuration. Consult your plan administrator if you are unsure which type applies.

Time: 20-30 minutes

Step 3: Review Calculation Results ⭐⭐

Purpose: Verify calculation is correct before finalizing.

What Happens:

Review monthly benefit amount
Check calculation inputs
Verify benefit option
Confirm everything is accurate
Finalize calculation for retirement

Time: 10-15 minutes

Step 4: Confirm Retirement Details ⭐

Purpose: Final review of all retirement information.

What Happens:

Review member information
Verify retirement date
Confirm calculation results
Ensure all details are correct

Time: 5 minutes

Step 5: Set Up Payment Information ⭐⭐⭐⭐

Purpose: Configure how and how much the member will be paid.

What Happens:

Enter gross monthly payment amount
Set up deductions (taxes, insurance, etc.)
Enter bank/direct deposit information
Configure payment schedule
Verify net payment amount

Time: 15-20 minutes

Step 6: Complete & Finalize ⭐

Purpose: Complete the retirement and celebrate!

What Happens:

Final validation of all steps
Wizard completion
Confetti celebration 🎉
Member is officially retired in system
Ready for first payment

Time: 2-5 minutes

Open member's profile (Global Search 🔍)
Click the Retire Member button in the upper right corner of the member banner

⚠️ CRITICAL: The Retirement Wizard is accessed through the Retire Member button in the upper right corner of the member banner, NOT from Benefit Calculations.

Two Ways to Start/Resume the Retirement Wizard:

Method 1: From Member Profile (Start or Resume)

Open member's profile
Click the Retire Member button in the upper right corner of the member banner
Retirement Wizard launches or resumes

Method 2: From PensionPro+ Home Page (Resume Only)

Navigate to PensionPro+ Home Page
Scroll to Retirement Processes module
Locate member's unfinished retirement process
Click the process to resume

💡 Important: Benefit Calculations in the left navigation sidebar are used ONLY to estimate benefit amounts. They do NOT initiate the retirement process or change member status.

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Preparation:

Verify Everything First - Check all data before starting
Gather All Information - Have everything ready
Update Member Records - Ensure current before starting
Get Approvals Early - Board, supervisor, etc.
Schedule Adequate Time - Don't rush, allow 1-2 hours

During Wizard:

Read Each Step Carefully - Don't skip instructions
Verify As You Go - Check each field
Complete Steps Fully - Finish current step before pausing
Add Detailed Notes - Explain your work
Save Frequently - Complete steps to trigger save
Ask Questions - Consult supervisor if unsure
Double-Check Critical Fields - Bank info, benefit amount, dates

Calculations:

Review Calculation Results - Ensure accurate
Compare to Estimates - Should match expectations
Verify Benefit Option - Member's actual selection
Confirm Beneficiary Info - Spelling, DOB, etc.

Payment Setup:

Read Bank Info Back to Member - Verify accuracy
Explain Net vs. Gross - Help member understand
Recommend Tax Withholding - Avoid year-end surprise
Verify Insurance Premiums - Current rates
Calculate Net Payment - Show member take-home

Completion:

Final Review - Check everything one more time
Notify Member - Confirm completion
Provide Documentation - Give member copies
Set Expectations - Explain first payment timing
Follow Up - Check first payment processes correctly

Documentation:

Add Comprehensive Memos - Document what you did
Save Supporting Documents - Attach to member record
Print Summary - For member file
Keep Audit Trail - Record decisions and approvals

❌ DON'T:

Preparation:

Don't Start Without Complete Data - Will cause problems
Don't Rush - Mistakes are costly
Don't Skip Verification - Check everything
Don't Ignore Missing Approvals - Get them first

During Wizard:

Don't Make Assumptions - Verify facts
Don't Skip Steps - Follow process completely
Don't Ignore Warnings - Investigate all alerts
Don't Forget to Save - Complete steps
Don't Leave Unclear Notes - Be specific
Don't Guess - Ask if unsure

Calculations:

Don't Proceed with Errors - Fix first
Don't Accept Unreasonable Results - Investigate
Don't Use Wrong Calculation Type - Verify correct type
Don't Forget Beneficiary Info - Complete for survivor options

Payment Setup:

Don't Transpose Bank Numbers - Verify carefully
Don't Skip Deductions - Member may want them
Don't Forget Tax Withholding - Explain importance
Don't Assume Payment Methods - Ask member

Completion:

Don't Complete Without Review - Final check crucial
Don't Complete Without Authority - Get required approvals
Don't Forget to Notify Member - Keep them informed
Don't Complete Wrong Wizard - Verify member

Post-Completion:

Don't Forget Follow-Up - Monitor first payment
Don't Ignore Member Questions - Respond promptly
Don't Forget Documentation - File everything
Common Questions

Q: How long does the retirement wizard take? A: 30-45 minutes if all information is ready. Can take longer for complex cases or if gathering information during process.

Q: Can I save my progress and come back later? A: Yes! The wizard auto-saves after each completed step. You can resume anytime.

Q: What if I make a mistake? A: If discovered early, cancel wizard, fix data, restart. If after completion, may need correction process - consult supervisor.

Q: Can I skip steps? A: No, must complete steps in order. Some steps may be auto-skipped if not applicable (e.g., status change if already retired).

Q: What if member isn't sure about benefit option? A: Pause the wizard, run comparison calculations, counsel member, resume when decided.

Q: Do I need supervisor approval? A: Depends on your organization. Some require review before completion, some after. Follow your procedures.

Q: What if calculation seems wrong? A: Stop immediately. Don't complete wizard. Investigate, verify inputs, consult supervisor, fix if needed.

Q: Can I edit after completing wizard? A: No, completed retirements are locked. Changes require correction procedures. Get it right the first time!

Q: What if bank information is wrong after completion? A: Can usually update payment info separately. See
Update Payment Information
.

Q: When will first payment be issued? A: Depends on payroll schedule, usually within first 1-2 pay cycles after completion. Direct deposit requires pre-note period.

Q: Can two people work on the same wizard? A: Only one person can have it open at a time, but can hand off with good notes. Not recommended to work simultaneously.

Q: What's the confetti celebration? A: Fun animation when wizard is completed! Acknowledges this major accomplishment. 🎉

Q: Can I test the wizard? A: Not with real member data! Use test environment or training database if available. Never complete test wizards in production.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't Start Wizard

Possible Causes:

Member not eligible
Already has in-progress wizard
System permissions
Member status incorrect

Solutions:

Check member eligibility (age, service)
Look for existing wizard - resume instead
Verify your permissions
Check member current status
Contact supervisor if member should be eligible
Issue: Calculation Step Won't Save

Possible Causes:

Calculation incomplete
Validation errors
Required fields missing
Calculation didn't run successfully

Solutions:

Ensure calculation completed successfully
Look for error messages
Verify all required fields filled
Re-run calculation if needed
Check for warnings that might block save
Issue: Payment Setup Shows Errors

Common Errors:

"Bank routing number invalid"
"Account number required"
"Total deductions exceed benefit"

Solutions:

Verify bank routing number (9 digits, valid bank)
Ensure account number entered
Check deductions don't exceed benefit
Verify all required payment fields completed
Check field format requirements
Issue: Can't Complete Wizard

Possible Causes:

Validation failures
Required steps incomplete
Missing documentation
System error

Solutions:

Review validation messages carefully
Go back through each step
Ensure all steps show complete/green checkmark
Verify all required documentation attached
Try refreshing page
Contact system support if technical issue
Issue: Completed Wrong Member by Accident

Immediate Actions:

Do NOT panic
Note exactly what happened
Notify supervisor immediately
Document the error
Follow correction procedures
May need to:
Reverse completed retirement
Start correct member retirement
Adjust both member records

Prevention:

Always verify member ID at start
Check member name at each step
Final confirmation before completing
Issue: Wizard Disappeared / Can't Find In-Progress Wizard

Solutions:

Check "All Wizards" not just "My Wizards"
Search by member name
Check if someone else completed it
Look in member's retirement history
Check audit log
May have been cancelled - check for memo
Contact system administrator if truly missing
