How Do I Run a Pension Calculation?

Category: Retirement and Benefits Time: 10-30 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐⭐ Advanced

Before You Begin:

Member's service history must be complete and accurate
Member profile page is open and accessible
Member is eligible for one of the calculation types
Recent salary information should be updated
Understand what type of calculation is needed
You understand which retirement situation applies to the member
Have member's retirement date and benefit option preferences
Basic member information is complete

Related Topics:

Retire a Member
Review Calculation Inputs and Outputs
Save and Share Calculation Results
What This Does

Provides a quick way to run a retirement benefit calculation and review the results. This process allows you to select the appropriate calculation type based on the member's specific retirement situation, enter the necessary information, and generate an estimate of their retirement benefit.

Calculates the precise monthly pension benefit amount a member will receive based on their years of service, salary history, retirement date, and benefit options. These calculations provide detailed benefit estimates for member counseling, planning, and board review.

⚠️ Important: Benefit Calculations are used ONLY to estimate or calculate benefit amounts. They do NOT change a member's status to retired, initiate the retirement process, or launch the Retirement Wizard. To actually retire a member, you must use the Status dropdown (see
Retire a Member
).

Quick Steps
Open member's profile
Click Benefit Calculations navigation card
Select calculation type
Enter/verify all required parameters
Review system-populated data
Click Calculate
Review results for accuracy
Save calculation
Share with member if appropriate
Calculation Types Explained

PensionPro+ supports 7 primary calculation types:

1. Superannuation (Options A, B, C)

What It Is: Standard retirement calculation for members who meet normal retirement eligibility.

When to Use:

Member meets age AND service requirements
Normal, non-disability retirement
Most common calculation type

Typical Eligibility:

Age 60 with 10+ years service
Age 55 with 25+ years service
Or as defined by your plan rules

Benefit Options:

Option A: Single Life (maximum benefit)
Option B: Modified Joint & Survivor
Option C: Full Joint & Survivor
2. Superannuation Option D

What It Is: Enhanced survivor benefit option with higher reduction during member's life but better protection for beneficiary.

When to Use:

Member wants maximum survivor protection
Willing to accept larger reduction for enhanced benefit
Qualified retirement with survivor election

Key Feature:

Higher survivor percentage
Larger reduction than Options B or C
Beneficiary continues to receive substantial benefit
3. Accidental Disability

What It Is: Disability benefits for members injured in the line of duty.

When to Use:

Member disabled due to job-related accident
Injury occurred while performing duties
Medical board approval obtained

Benefit Features:

Usually 2/3 of final salary
Service time may not be primary factor
Higher than ordinary disability
May continue until recovery or retirement age

Requirements:

Medical documentation
Proof of on-duty injury
Board approval
4. Ordinary Disability (Non-Veteran)

What It Is: Disability benefits for non-veteran members with non-job-related disability.

When to Use:

Member disabled but not due to job
Member is not a veteran
Meets plan's disability criteria

Benefit Features:

Lower than accidental disability
Based on service time with enhanced multiplier
Minimum service requirements may apply

Requirements:

Medical examination
Proof of disability
Minimum service (often 10 years)
5. Ordinary Disability (Veteran)

What It Is: Disability benefits for veteran members with non-job-related disability.

When to Use:

Member disabled but not due to job
Member has military veteran status
Meets plan's disability criteria

Benefit Features:

Similar to non-veteran ordinary disability
May have veteran-specific enhancements
Recognition of military service

Requirements:

Proof of veteran status
Medical examination
Disability documentation
6. Termination Allowance

What It Is: Benefit for members leaving employment before retirement eligible.

When to Use:

Member terminating employment
Does not meet retirement eligibility
Vested in plan (usually 10+ years)

Benefit Options:

Deferred benefit (paid at retirement age)
Lump sum withdrawal
Rollover to another plan

Key Points:

Lower than retirement benefit
May require waiting period
Return of contributions option
7. Accidental Death

What It Is: Survivor benefits when active member dies in line of duty.

When to Use:

Member died while performing job duties
Death caused by job-related accident
Calculating benefits for survivors

Benefit Features:

Enhanced death benefit
Immediate benefit to survivors
Usually higher than normal death benefit

Beneficiaries:

Spouse
Children
Named beneficiaries
As per plan rules

✅ Run Full Calculations For:

Need board approval
Setting up actual payments

Pre-Retirement Counseling

Member within 2 years of retirement
Wants detailed, accurate projections
Comparing multiple options formally

Disability Processing

Medical board approved disability
Need official benefit determination

Board Presentations

Retirement packets
Official approvals
Trustee review

Member Disputes/Appeals

Need verified, documented calculation
Legal or formal inquiry
Audit purposes

Setting actual payment amount

Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Access Calculation Tools

From Member Profile:

Find and Open Member

Use Global Search (🔍)
Or navigate via Member Directory
Click member name to open profile

Go to Calculations Section

Look for Benefit Calculations card on the left navigation
Click Benefit Calculations navigation card

Start New Calculation

Click New Calculation button
Or Add Calculation
Or Calculate Benefits
Step 2: Select Calculation Type

Calculation Type Selection Screen:

Review Available Types

System displays all calculation types
Some may be grayed out (not eligible)

Select Appropriate Type

 

For Normal Retirements:

✅ Superannuation (Options A, B, C) - Most common

For Special Retirements:

Superannuation Option D - Enhanced survivor

For Disabilities:

Accidental Disability - Job-related injury
Ordinary Disability (Non-Vet) - Non-job disability
Ordinary Disability (Veteran) - Veteran with disability

For Terminations:

Termination Allowance - Leaving before eligible

For Death Benefits:

Accidental Death - Line-of-duty death

Click Select or Continue

💡 Not Sure Which Type? Most retirements use "Superannuation (Options A, B, C)."

Step 3: Enter/Verify Member Information

Member Demographics:

System typically auto-populates from member record:

Name: Verify correct member
Member ID: Double-check ID
Date of Birth: Confirm accuracy
Social Security Number: Verify if shown
Gender: Confirm (may affect some calculations)

If Any Information is Wrong:

❌ Don't proceed with calculation
✅ Exit and update member demographic information first
✅ Then return to start calculation
Step 4: Enter Retirement Date and Age Information

Retirement Date:

Select Date: Usually first of month
Click calendar to choose
Or type: MM/DD/YYYY

💡 Tip: Retirement typically begins first of month following last day of work.

Age at Retirement:

Usually auto-calculates from DOB and retirement date
Verify it's correct
Important for eligibility determination

Years and Months to Retirement:

If retiring in future, shows time until retirement
Verify makes sense with selected date
Step 5: Enter/Verify Service Time

Years of Creditable Service:

System should pull from employment history:

Regular Service:

Employment periods from history
System calculates total years and months

Check Carefully:

✅ All employment periods included
✅ Breaks in service properly excluded
✅ Part-time service calculated correctly

Purchased Service:

Additional service member bought
Should auto-populate if in system
Verify amount is correct
Confirm fully paid

Military Service Credit:

Military buyback service
Should be in system if purchased
Verify included in calculation

Total Creditable Service:

Sum of all service types
This is critical number for calculation
Verify it matches your understanding

⚠️ If Service Time Looks Wrong:

Stop and investigate
Review employment history
Update records if needed
Re-start calculation
Step 6: Enter/Verify Salary Information

Final Average Salary (FAS):

Critical component of benefit calculation.

System Calculates FAS:

Based on plan rules (typically):

Last 3 years average
Last 5 years average
Highest 3 years in last 10
Other plan-specific formulas

Review FAS Breakdown:

System may show:

2023: $68,500
2024: $70,200
2025: $72,000
---------------
Total: $210,700
Average: $70,233


Verify:

✅ Salary years are correct
✅ Amounts match payroll records
✅ Recent raises included
✅ Only pensionable compensation included

Pensionable vs. Non-Pensionable:

Usually Included:

✅ Base salary
✅ Regular wages
✅ Longevity pay (if applicable)
✅ Shift differential (if applicable)

Usually Excluded:

❌ Overtime (typically)
❌ Bonuses
❌ Reimbursements
❌ Non-pensionable stipends

💡 If Salary Needs Update:

May need to update payroll/salary records
Then re-run calculation
FAS should auto-recalculate
Step 7: Select Benefit Option

For Superannuation Calculations:

Option A: Single Life (Maximum Benefit)

Highest monthly payment
No survivor benefit
Payments end at member's death
Best for: Single members or those with other survivor income

Option B: 50% Joint & Survivor

Reduced monthly payment during life
50% continues to beneficiary after death
Balance of benefit and survivor protection
Most popular option

Option C: 100% Joint & Survivor

More reduced monthly payment
100% continues to beneficiary after death
Maximum survivor protection
Beneficiary receives same amount

Option D: Enhanced Survivor

Highest reduction
Enhanced survivor benefit
Special provisions
For maximum survivor coverage

If Survivor Option Selected:

Must provide beneficiary information:

Name
Date of Birth (affects calculation)
Relationship
Contact information
Step 8: Enter Additional Parameters

Depending on Calculation Type:

For Disability Calculations:

Disability type (accident vs. ordinary)
Date of disability
Medical board approval date
Percentage of disability
Expected duration

For Death Benefits:

Date of death
Cause of death
On-duty verification
Survivor information

For Termination Allowance:

Last day of work
Vested status
Payout option preference
Rollover information (if applicable)

Plan-Specific Fields:

Your system may have additional fields:

Hazardous duty status
Special provisions
Cost of living adjustments
Other plan-specific factors
Step 9: Review All Inputs

Before Calculating:

Use the Input Review Screen (if available):

Go through systematically:

Member information correct
Retirement date is intended date
Service time verified
Salary information current
Benefit option selected
Beneficiary info complete (if needed)
All required fields filled
No error messages

💡 Take Your Time: Accurate inputs = accurate calculation. Rushing leads to errors.

Step 10: Run the Calculation

Execute Calculation:

Click Calculate Button

Usually blue button at bottom
May say "Calculate," "Run Calculation," or "Calculate Benefits"

Wait for Processing

System performs calculations
May take 10-30 seconds
Progress indicator may appear
Do not close window or navigate away

Calculation Completes

Results screen appears
Green success message
Or error/warning messages if issues

Situation: Member retiring next month. Need official calculation for board packet.

Process:

Verify all member information is current
Open calculations for member
Select "New Calculation"
Choose "Superannuation (Options A, B, C)"
Verify retirement date: First of next month
Review service time: 27 years, 3 months - Correct ✅
Review FAS: $71,450 - Matches recent payroll ✅
Member selected: Option B (50% Survivor)
Enter wife's DOB for beneficiary
Review all inputs
Click Calculate
Result: $4,021.83/month
Review looks good
Save with label "Final - For Board Approval Feb 2026"
Export to PDF
Add to board packet
Add memo to member record documenting calculation
Schedule member notification after board approval

Timeline: 25-30 minutes

Scenario 3: Accidental Disability Calculation

Situation: Officer injured in line of duty. Medical board approved disability retirement.

Start new calculation

Scenario 4: Catching and Correcting Calculation Error

Situation: Ran calculation, result seems too high. Need to investigate.

Process:

Calculation shows $5,200/month
Member has 28 years service, $70,000 salary
Expected around $4,100/month
Review inputs carefully
Service time shows: 38 years
Found issue: Purchased service counted twice
Regular: 25 years
Purchased: 3 years
System somehow added purchased service as 10 additional years
Exit calculation (don't save)
Review employment history
Find data entry error in purchased service record
Correct purchased service record: 3 years, not 10
Start new calculation
Now service shows correctly: 28 years
Recalculate
New result: $4,083/month
This matches expected range ✅
Save corrected calculation
Add detailed note explaining error was found and corrected
Document what was wrong and how fixed
Review similar recent calculations to ensure no other errors

Timeline: 45-60 minutes (investigation + correction)

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Verify Before You Calculate - Check all inputs first
Use Current Data - Ensure salary and service are up-to-date
Review Employment History - Confirm service time is complete
Double-Check Critical Inputs - Service, salary, dates
Investigate Warnings - Don't ignore system alerts
Save Every Calculation - Create permanent record
Add Detailed Notes - Context helps future review
Compare to Similar Cases - Does result seem reasonable?
Get Second Review - Have colleague verify important calculations
Keep Member Informed - Share appropriate calculations
Document Everything - Record what calculations you ran and why
Follow Up - Re-run if member information changes

❌ DON'T:

Rush Through Inputs - Accuracy is critical
Skip Verification - Always check service and salary
Ignore Error Messages - Fix issues before continuing
Assume Data is Current - Verify recent changes included
Share Without Review - Verify calculation before sending
Delete Old Calculations - Keep historical record
Forget to Save - Always save final calculations
Make Promises - Calculations can change
Use Outdated Information - Update records first
Skip Documentation - Always note what and why
Common Questions

Q: How long does a calculation take? A: Entering information: 15-20 minutes. Running calculation: 10-30 seconds. Total: 20-30 minutes for thorough work.

Q: Can I save a calculation and return to edit it? A: No, saved calculations are locked for audit integrity. If you need to change inputs, run a new calculation and clearly label it as revised.

Q: What if member's salary just changed? A: Update the salary in their record first, then run the calculation. The system will use the current salary information.

Q: Do I need to run separate calculations for each benefit option? A: Depends on your system. Some show all options in one calculation. Others require running separate calculations for each option. Best practice: run separate calculations for member comparison.

Q: What if calculation result doesn't seem right? A: Stop and investigate. Review inputs carefully. Compare to similar members. Check for data entry errors. Don't proceed with questionable calculations.

Q: How often should calculations be re-run? A: Whenever significant information changes (salary increase, service purchase, etc.). Always run fresh calculation at actual retirement.

Q: Can member run their own calculations? A: Some systems offer member self-service estimates. Official calculations should be run by trained staff.

Q: What's the difference between draft and final calculations? A: Draft = preliminary, may change. Final = reviewed and approved, used for actual retirement processing.

Q: Do calculations expire? A: They don't expire but may become outdated if member information changes or plan rules change. Re-run if more than 6 months old.

Troubleshooting
Issue: System Won't Let Me Select Calculation Type

Cause: Member may not meet eligibility for that type.

Solutions:

Check member age and service
Verify member status
Review plan eligibility requirements
Try different calculation type
Contact supervisor if member should be eligible
Issue: Service Time Seems Wrong

Solutions:

Review employment history in member record
Check for missing employment periods
Verify purchased service is recorded
Check for breaks in service
Update history records if needed
Close calculation and restart after corrections
Issue: Calculation Gives Error Message

Common Errors:

"Member does not meet eligibility"
"Required information missing"
"Invalid date"

Solutions:

Read error message carefully
Fix the specific issue mentioned
Verify all required fields completed
Check dates are logical
Confirm member meets eligibility
Contact support if error seems wrong
Issue: Can't Save Calculation

Solutions:

Ensure calculation completed successfully
Check for validation errors
Verify all required fields filled
Try adding calculation name/notes
Refresh page and try again
Contact support if problem persists
