How Do I Run and Export Benefit Calculations?

Category: Retirement and Benefits Time: 10-15 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

Member's profile must be open
You have access to the member's profile in PensionPro+
You know which retirement benefit option to calculate (Superannuation A, B, C, etc.)
Have relevant calculation information ready (retirement date, overrides, etc.)
Understand which calculation type is needed
You have the appropriate permissions to save calculations
You understand the difference between Document Center and Report Center access

Related Topics:

Review Calculation Inputs and Outputs
What This Does

Walks you through running benefit calculations for members and saving the results in multiple ways. You can download calculations directly to your computer, save them to the system-wide Report Center for administrative access, or save them to the member's Document Center so they can view results through the Employee Self-Service (ESS) Portal.

Helps you run benefit calculations for members (retirement, disability, death benefits, etc.) and export the results to PDF or Word format. You can download calculations to your computer or save them to Report Center or Document Center for future reference.

⚠️ Important: Benefit Calculations are used ONLY to estimate or calculate a member's retirement benefit amounts. They do NOT change a member's status to retired, initiate the retirement process, or launch the Retirement Wizard. To retire a member, see
Retire a Member
.

Quick Steps
Open member's profile
Click Benefit Calculations in navigation sidebar
Select the calculation type you need
Enter calculation parameters in the modal
Review calculation results
Click Export to save or download
Choose worksheet type, format, and destination
Understanding Benefit Calculations

Available Calculation Types:

The system offers several calculation types depending on your member's situation:

Superannuation A, B & C - Standard retirement calculations
Option D - Survivor benefit calculations
Accidental Disability - Accidental disability benefits
Accidental Death - Death benefit calculations
Ordinary Disability (Non Veteran) - Regular disability benefits
Ordinary Disability (Veteran) - Veteran disability benefits
Term Allowance - Termination allowance calculations
Retirement Calculation - Comprehensive retirement benefits

💡 Note: Available calculations may vary based on your system configuration and member eligibility.

Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Access Benefit Calculations

Navigate to Calculations:

Open the member's profile
Look for Benefit Calculations in the left navigation sidebar
Click Benefit Calculations
View the list of available calculation types

💡 Note: The Benefit Calculations section is for estimating benefit amounts only. It does NOT retire members or start the Retirement Wizard. To actually retire a member:

Click the Status dropdown in the top member information banner → Select Retire Member
OR from PensionPro+ Home Page → Retirement Processes module → Resume an unfinished process

Select Calculation Type:

Choose the appropriate calculation based on member's needs:

Retirement → Superannuation A, B & C or Retirement Calculation
Survivor benefits → Option D
Disability → Accidental Disability or Ordinary Disability
Death benefits → Accidental Death
Termination → Term Allowance
Step 2: Enter Calculation Parameters

When you click a calculation type:

A modal window opens with input fields specific to that calculation.

Common Input Fields (varies by calculation type):

Required Fields:

Retirement Date - Date member will retire or benefit begins
Spouse/Non-Spouse Flag - Whether beneficiary is spouse or non-spouse
Salary Information - Current or projected salary

Optional Override Fields:

Service Override - Override calculated years of service
Age Override - Override member's age for calculation
Accumulated Deductions Override - Override total deductions
Final Annual Salary - Override final salary amount
Average High Earnings - Override high-3 or high-5 average
Anti-Spiking - Apply anti-spiking rules
Section 18 - Apply section 18 provisions

Description Field:

Add notes about the calculation purpose
Text appears on calculation worksheet
Example: "Pre-retirement estimate for counseling session 1/15/26"

Complete the Form:

Fill in all required fields (marked with red asterisk)
Add any overrides if needed to adjust calculations
Enter description for documentation purposes
Click Submit button

💡 Tip: Use suggested retirement date buttons when available to quickly select common retirement ages.

Step 3: Review Calculation Results

After Submitting:

The calculation results display automatically showing:

Results Tabs:

Calculation Summary - Final benefit amounts and comparisons
Calculation Parameters - All inputs and overrides used
Member Data - Member information used in calculation

What You'll See:

Monthly and annual benefit amounts
Comparison of different options (if applicable)
Calculation methodology and formulas
Service time, salary, and age factors
Any special adjustments or limitations

Action Buttons:

Re-Calculate - Adjust parameters and run calculation again
Export - Download or save calculation results

Review for Accuracy:

Before exporting, verify:

✅ Member name and ID are correct
✅ Retirement/benefit date is correct
✅ Service years look reasonable
✅ Benefit amounts are in expected range
✅ All required overrides were applied
✅ No error messages displayed
Step 4: Export Calculation

Click the Export Button:

After reviewing results, click Export button in top-right of results screen
Export modal opens with configuration options

Step 1: Select Worksheet and Format

Worksheet Type:

Choose which worksheet format to export:

Long - Detailed calculation with all fields and breakdowns
Short - Summary calculation with key results only
Options - Comparison of multiple benefit options (if available)

💡 Note: Available worksheet types vary by calculation. Some calculations only offer Long and Short worksheets.

File Format:

Choose output format:

PDF - Best for printing and sharing (recommended)
DOCX - Microsoft Word format for editing

Step 2: Select Action

Choose what to do with the exported calculation:

1. Download Calculation

Downloads file directly to your computer
Best for immediate use or sharing via email
File saved to your browser's download folder

2. Save to Report Center

Saves calculation to Report Center
Admin-level access only
Use for official records and board reports

3. Save to Document Center

Saves calculation to member's Document Center
Visible to the member if they have portal access
Use for sharing finalized calculations with members

⚠️ Important: Document Center files may be visible to members through member self-service portal.

Complete Export:

Select worksheet type (Long, Short, or Options)
Select format (PDF or DOCX)
Choose action (Download, Report Center, or Document Center)
Click action button (e.g., "Download", "Save to Report Center")
Wait for confirmation message
Step 5: Re-Run Calculations (Optional)

To Run with Different Parameters:

If you need to try different scenarios:

Click Re-Calculate button
Modal reopens with previous values pre-filled
Adjust parameters (retirement date, overrides, etc.)
Click Submit to generate new results
Review and export new calculation

Common Reasons to Re-Run:

Compare different retirement dates
Test impact of salary changes
Try different benefit options
Correct data entry errors
Update with new member information

💡 Tip: Keep notes on which scenarios you've tested to share with member during counseling.

Common Scenarios
Scenario 1: Running Basic Retirement Estimate

Situation: Member calls asking about their retirement benefit for next year.

Steps:

Open member's profile
Click Benefit Calculations → Superannuation A, B & C
Modal opens
Enter retirement date (e.g., 1/1/2027)
System auto-populates service and salary
Add description: "Phone inquiry 1/14/26 - estimate for 1/1/27 retirement"
Click Submit
Review results showing benefit amounts
Click Export
Select: Long worksheet, PDF format, Download
Click Download
Share PDF with member via email

Timeline: 5-10 minutes

Scenario 2: Comparing Multiple Retirement Dates

Situation: Member wants to compare retiring now vs. in 2 years.

Steps:

Run first calculation for current date
Export and save to computer as "Retire_Now_2026.pdf"
Click Re-Calculate button
Change retirement date to 2 years from now
Update description: "Retire in 2 years - comparison"
Click Submit
Review increased benefit amount
Export and save as "Retire_2028.pdf"
Open both PDFs side-by-side
Discuss pros/cons with member

Timeline: 15-20 minutes

Scenario 3: Saving Calculation for Member Portal

Situation: Member wants access to their calculation through the member portal.

Steps:

Run calculation with accurate final data
Review results carefully - this will be visible to member
Click Export
Select: Long worksheet, PDF format
Choose Save to Document Center
Click Save to Document Center
Success message confirms save
Inform member calculation is available in their portal
Member can log in and view under Documents

Timeline: 10 minutes

Scenario 4: Official Calculation for Board Review

Situation: Board requires calculation for retirement approval.

Steps:

Verify all member data is current and accurate
Run calculation with exact retirement date
Add description: "Official calculation for Board approval - [Member Name] - [Date]"
Review all results thoroughly
Click Export
Select: Long worksheet, PDF format
Choose Save to Report Center
Click Save to Report Center
Success message confirms save
Retrieve from Report Center for board packet
Also download copy for member's paper file

Timeline: 15-20 minutes

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Verify Member Data First - Check service history and salary before calculating
Use Descriptive Notes - Add context in description field for future reference
Review Before Exporting - Double-check all results before saving
Save Official Calculations - Use Report Center for formal calculations
Use Consistent Naming - If downloading, use clear file names (e.g. include member name and calculation type)
Keep Member Informed - Explain calculation assumptions and limitations
Test Multiple Scenarios - Help members compare options
Document Your Work - Add calculation description explaining purpose

❌ DON'T:

Rush the Input - Take time to enter accurate parameters
Skip Review - Always verify results before sharing
Save to Document Center Without Member Knowledge - Inform members when saving to their documents
Share Preliminary Calculations as Final - Label estimates clearly
Forget to Export - Calculations may not be permanently saved until exported
Use Vague Descriptions - Be specific about calculation purpose and date
Ignore Warnings - Address any system warnings or alerts
Understanding Export Options
Worksheet Types Explained

Long Worksheet:

Most comprehensive format
Includes all calculation details
Shows all input parameters
Displays full formulas and breakdowns
Best for official records and detailed review
Use for: Board packets, official retirements, audit documentation

Short Worksheet:

Summary format with key results
Excludes detailed formulas and intermediate steps
Easier for members to understand
Faster to review
Best for member communications and quick reference
Use for: Member estimates, phone inquiries, counseling sessions

Options Worksheet (when available):

Side-by-side comparison of benefit options
Shows trade-offs between choices
Helps members make informed decisions
Not available for all calculation types
Best for comparing scenarios
Use for: Comparing retirement dates, benefit options, survivor benefits
Format Types Explained

PDF Format:

✅ Universal compatibility - opens on any device
✅ Preserves formatting exactly
✅ Cannot be easily altered
✅ Professional appearance
✅ Smaller file size
❌ Not editable
Best for: Sharing with members, printing, official records

DOCX Format:

✅ Editable in Microsoft Word
✅ Can add notes or annotations
✅ Useful for creating custom reports
❌ Requires Microsoft Word or compatible software
❌ Formatting may shift if edited
❌ Larger file size
Best for: Internal use, creating custom reports, adding annotations

💡 Recommendation: Use PDF for 95% of exports. Only use DOCX if you specifically need to edit the document.

Destination Options Explained

Download:

File saves to your computer
Full control over where file is stored
Can share via email, print, or upload elsewhere
Not automatically backed up in system
Fastest option
Use when: Immediate need, emailing to member, printing for paper file

Report Center:

Saves to system's report repository
Admin-level storage
Not visible to members
Organized by date and type
Accessible to authorized staff
Permanent record
Use when: Official calculations, board packets, audit records, final retirements

Document Center:

Saves to member's document collection
May be visible to member through member portal
Organized under member's profile
Easy access for staff working with member
Member can download if portal access enabled
Use when: Sharing finalized calculations with members, member-facing documents
Troubleshooting
Issue: Calculation Won't Submit

Possible Causes:

Missing required fields
Invalid data in input fields
System validation errors

Solutions:

Check for red asterisk (*) next to required fields
Verify dates are in correct format (MM/DD/YYYY)
Ensure numeric fields don't have invalid characters
Look for error messages at top of modal
Try clearing and re-entering problematic fields
Refresh page and try again if persistent
Issue: Export Button Not Appearing

Possible Causes:

Calculation hasn't completed yet
No calculation results loaded
Permission issues

Solutions:

Verify calculation actually ran (results should be visible)
Check if "Re-Calculate" button is present (means calculation completed)
Verify you have export permissions
Try refreshing the page
Contact system administrator if permission issue suspected
Issue: Wrong Worksheet Type Options

Possible Causes:

Some calculations don't support all worksheet types
System configuration limits available options

Solutions:

This is normal behavior - not all calculations offer all worksheet types
Use available worksheet types (typically Long and Short are always available)
If Options worksheet not available, run multiple calculations to compare scenarios
Issue: Can't Find Saved Calculation

Possible Causes:

Saved to wrong location
Didn't wait for confirmation
Export failed but no error shown

Solutions:

If saved to Download:

Check browser's download folder
Look for recent PDF/DOCX files
Check browser download history

If saved to Report Center:

Navigate to Report Center from main menu
Filter by date and calculation type
Check permissions - may need admin access
Verify success message appeared after export

If saved to Document Center:

Go to member's profile
Click Document Center section
Sort by date (newest first)
Check file name matches calculation type
Issue: Results Look Wrong

Possible Causes:

Incorrect input parameters
Wrong calculation type selected
Outdated member data
System calculation error

Solutions:

Click Re-Calculate and verify all inputs
Check retirement date is correct
Verify service years and salary make sense
Review any overrides you entered
Check member's base data (service history, salary history)
Compare to manual calculation or previous estimates
If truly incorrect, contact system administrator with details

Common Questions

Q: Do calculations save automatically? A: No. You must click Export and choose a save destination (Report Center or Document Center) to permanently save calculations. Downloads only save to your computer.

Q: Can members see calculations saved to Document Center? A: Potentially yes. If your system has a member self-service portal and the member has access, they can view documents saved to their Document Center. Use Report Center for internal-only calculations.

Q: How many times can I re-run a calculation? A: Unlimited. Click Re-Calculate as many times as needed to test different scenarios.

Q: What's the difference between Report Center and Document Center? A: Report Center is admin-only storage for official records. Document Center is member-specific storage that may be visible to the member through a portal.

Q: Can I edit a calculation after it's exported? A: No. Calculations are snapshots at point-in-time. To make changes, click Re-Calculate to run a new calculation with updated parameters.

Q: Which worksheet type should I use? A: Use Long for official/detailed records, Short for member communications, Options for scenario comparisons (when available).

Q: What if I need a calculation type not shown in the sidebar? A: Available calculations depend on your system configuration and member eligibility. Contact your system administrator if you need access to additional calculation types.

Q: Do I need to save every calculation I run? A: No. Save calculations that might be referenced later - official retirements, board submissions, member estimates. Test calculations for learning purposes don't need to be saved.

Q: Can I email calculations directly from the system? A: No direct email feature. Download the PDF and attach to your email, or save to Document Center and notify member to access through portal.

Q: How long are exported calculations retained? A: Follow your organization's document retention policy. Calculations in Report Center and Document Center are typically retained indefinitely unless manually deleted.
