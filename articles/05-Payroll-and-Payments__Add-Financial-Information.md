How Do I Add Financial Information?

Category: Payroll and Payments Time: 10-15 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

Have member's payment method selection (check or direct deposit)
If direct deposit: Have bank account and routing numbers
Have deduction information (insurance, tax withholding, etc.)
Verify member status is appropriate for payment setup

Related Topics:

Update Payment Information
Change Checking Bank Account Details
Update Federal Tax Withholdings

What This Does

Sets up and manages a member's complete financial information for pension payment processing. This includes payment method (check or direct deposit), bank account details, deductions (insurance, tax withholding, garnishments), and payment preferences.

Complete financial information is required before a member can receive pension payments through the regular payroll process.

Quick Steps
Open member's profile
Click Financial Info navigation card → Payment Information section
Click on an existing payment record to edit, OR click + Payment to add new
Review and update Retirement Information tab (if necessary)
Update Payment Information tab - Select payment method (check or direct deposit)
If direct deposit: Enter bank information
Update Amounts and Deductions tab - Add deductions (insurance, taxes, etc.)
Update Taxes tab - Set up tax withholding
Click Save
Verify information complete for payroll
Step-by-Step Instructions
Step 1: Navigate to Financial Information

Where to Go:

Find and open the member's profile
Click Financial Info navigation card
Look for Payment Information section
Current financial setup displays (or empty if not set up yet)
Step 2: Open Payment Information Edit Window

Navigate to Payment Information:

Either scroll down to the Payment Information section, OR
Click Payment Info in the left-side Financial Info navigation

What You'll See:

A table displaying existing payment records with columns:

Payment Type
Payee
Annuity
Pension
Net Payment
Unit

To Edit or Add:

To edit an existing payment: Click directly on the payment row you want to edit
To add a new payment: Click the + Payment button in the top-right header

💡 Note: There is no separate "Edit" button. You must click directly on the payment record to open the edit window.

The Payment Information Edit window opens with four tabs:

Retirement Information - Calculation selection, payroll settings
Payment Information - Payment method, bank account details
Amounts and Deductions - Payment amounts and deductions
Taxes - Tax withholding settings

⚠️ Important: Always review the Retirement Information tab first before editing other tabs. This tab contains critical information about the member's retirement calculation and payroll status.

Step 3: Review Retirement Information Tab (FIRST)

Before editing payment details, review this tab:

What You'll See:

Section	Fields
Calculation Details	Calculation Selection, As Of Date, Unit
Payroll Settings	Pay Status, Print Notice of Deposit, Lock Date, This Payment Only, 90 B, One Time Payment

What to Verify:

Calculation Selection - Correct retirement calculation is selected
As Of Date - Retirement effective date
Unit - Member's organizational unit
Pay Status - Should be active for regular payments
Lock Date - Payment lock status (if applicable)

When to Update:

If changing retirement calculation being used
If adjusting payroll status settings
If member's unit has changed
If setting up temporary payment locks

💡 Best Practice: Review this tab every time you edit payment information to ensure payroll settings are correct.

Step 4: Update Payment Information Tab

Click the "Payment Information" tab.

What to Do:

Step 1: Select Payment Type and Payee

Payment Type (required)

Select from dropdown
Options include: Check, Direct Deposit, EFT, and other system-configured types
Most common: "Check" or "Direct Deposit"

Payee (required)

Select who receives the payment
Usually the member themselves
May be beneficiary for survivor benefits
Format shows: "M - Member Name" or "B-ID - Beneficiary Name"

💡 Note: The payment type options available depend on your system configuration. Contact your system administrator if you need additional payment types.

Step 2: Enter Bank Information (if Direct Deposit)

Option 1: Paper Check

When to Use:

Member doesn't have bank account
Member prefers physical check
Member requests check mailed

What to Enter:

Select Payment Method: "Check"
Mailing Address:
Verify or enter check mailing address
Should match contact information unless member specifies different address
Check Name:
Name to appear on check
Usually member's legal name
May include "Estate of" for beneficiary payments

Check Delivery:

Checks mailed to address on file
Or held for pickup (per your organization's policy)
Member receives paper check each pay period

Step 2: Enter Bank Information (if Direct Deposit)

If Payment Type = "Direct Deposit":

The payment information tab shows two Direct Deposit Groups, allowing split deposits to multiple accounts.

Direct Deposit Group 1 (Primary Account):

ABA Number (Routing Number)

9-digit bank routing number
Found on bottom left of check
Format: 123456789
System validates routing number

Bank Name

Name of member's bank
Example: "First National Bank"
Optional but helpful

Account Type

Checking (most common)
Savings

Checking Account Number

Member's bank account number
Found on bottom of check (middle numbers)
Verify carefully - most common error source

Confirm Checking Account Number

Re-enter account number to verify
Must match exactly
Prevents typo errors

Payment Amount

Amount to deposit to this account
Leave blank for 100% to this account
Or enter specific amount for split deposit

Direct Deposit Group 2 (Secondary Account - Optional):

Same fields as Group 1. Use if member wants to split deposit between two accounts (e.g., checking and savings).

💡 Important: Have member provide a voided check or bank letter. Entering wrong account number sends money to wrong account!

If Payment Type = "Check":

No bank information is required. System will generate paper checks mailed to member's address on file.

Step 5: Update Amounts and Deductions Tab

Click the "Amounts and Deductions" tab.

Step 5: Update Amounts and Deductions Tab

Click the "Amounts and Deductions" tab.

What You'll See:

Section	Fields
Payment Amounts	Annuity, Pension, Pre 81 COLA, Post 81 COLA, Post 97 COLA, Dependent Allowance, Add Annuity, Vet Allowance, Chapter 389 Amount, Gross Payment, Net Payment
Tax Deductions	Federal Tax, State Tax
Other Deductions	Deduction 1-8 (Amount and Type), DOR Case Number

Payment Amounts Section:

These fields typically auto-populate from the member's retirement calculation. You may need to adjust them for:

Manual adjustments
Corrections
One-time payments

Common Deduction Types:

Health Insurance:

Find an empty deduction slot (Deduction 1-8)
Enter Amount: Monthly premium (e.g., 275.00)
Select Type: "Health Insurance" or specific plan

Dental Insurance:

Find an empty deduction slot
Enter Amount: Monthly premium (e.g., 45.00)
Select Type: "Dental Insurance"

Life Insurance:

Find an empty deduction slot
Enter Amount: Monthly premium
Select Type: "Life Insurance"

Garnishments:

Find an empty deduction slot
Enter Amount: As specified in court order (e.g., 500.00)
Select Type: Appropriate garnishment type
Enter DOR Case Number if applicable

Other Deductions:

Union dues
Pension loan repayments
Voluntary deductions
Custom organization-specific deductions
Step 6: Update Taxes Tab

Click the "Taxes" tab.

Federal Tax Withholding:

See
Update Federal Tax Withholdings
for detailed instructions.

Options typically include:

Flat dollar amount per payment
Percentage of gross payment
Based on member's W-4 form

State Tax Withholding:

If applicable in your state:

Enter amount or percentage
Based on state withholding form
Step 7: Review and Save

Final Checks:

Retirement Information Tab: ✓ Calculation selection is correct ✓ Payroll settings are appropriate ✓ Unit assignment is correct

Payment Information Tab:

For Direct Deposit: ✓ Routing number is 9 digits and validates ✓ Account number entered correctly twice ✓ Account type is correct (checking vs savings) ✓ Bank name entered ✓ Payment amounts total correctly (if split deposit)

For Check: ✓ Payment type is set to "Check" ✓ Payee is correct

Amounts and Deductions Tab: ✓ All applicable deductions added ✓ Deduction amounts are correct ✓ Deduction types are selected

Taxes Tab: ✓ Tax withholding configured (if desired) ✓ Amounts or percentages are correct

When Ready:

Click Save button at bottom of window
Confirmation message appears
Financial information is now set up
Member can be included in payroll
Understanding Financial Information Components
Payment Method Requirements

What's Required Before Member Can Be Paid:

Minimum Requirements:

Member status: "Retired" or eligible status
Payment method selected (check or direct deposit)
If direct deposit: Valid bank account and routing number
If check: Valid mailing address

Recommended:

Tax withholding set up (if member wants taxes withheld)
All applicable deductions added
Payment information verified with member

Optional:

Insurance deductions (if enrolled)
Voluntary deductions
Payment notes
Direct Deposit Validation

System Checks:

Routing Number:

Must be 9 digits
Must be valid US bank routing number
System validates against Federal Reserve database
Invalid routing number prevents save

Account Number:

Varies in length (typically 4-17 digits)
Both entries must match
System may mask for security
Incorrect account number causes payment to fail

Verification:

Some organizations do pre-note test
Small deposit sent to verify account
Confirm with member before first payment

Verify unit assignment
Ensure pay status is active

Deduction 1 Amount: 275.00

Situation: Retiree currently receives checks, wants to switch to direct deposit.

Steps:

Request voided check or bank letter from member

Click on the existing payment record to edit
Retirement Information tab: Review settings (no changes needed)
Payment Information tab:
Change Payment Type from "Check" to "Direct Deposit"
Enter bank information:
Routing number
Bank name
Account type: "Checking"
Account number
Confirm account number
Click Save
Inform member: "Direct deposit will begin with your [date] payment. You'll receive your last check for [previous month]."
Scan and attach voided check to member documents
Add memo documenting change
Scenario: Adding Insurance Deduction

Situation: Retiree enrolls in dental insurance, need to add deduction.

Steps:

Receive dental enrollment form
Open member profile → Financial Info → Payment Information
Click on the existing payment record to edit
Retirement Information tab: Review (no changes needed)
Amounts and Deductions tab:
Find an empty deduction slot
Deduction Amount: 45.00 (for $45/month premium)
Deduction Type: "Dental Insurance" or "Dental Plan Standard"
Click Save
Verify deduction added to payment information
Deduction will appear in next payroll
Add memo: "Dental insurance added - effective [date]"
Scenario: Setting Up Garnishment

Situation: Received court order for child support garnishment.

Steps:

Review court order carefully
Note: Garnishment amount, case number, payee information
Open member profile → Financial Info → Payment Information
Click on the existing payment record to edit
Retirement Information tab: Review (no changes needed)
Amounts and Deductions tab:
Find an empty deduction slot
Deduction Amount: As specified in court order (e.g., 500.00)
Deduction Type: "Garnishment - Child Support" (or appropriate type)
DOR Case Number: Enter case number if field is available
Click Save
Set up corresponding vendor payment for garnishment agency (separate process)
Scan and attach court order to member documents
Add memo: "Garnishment order received [date] - Case #[number]"
Monitor until garnishment satisfied

Important: Garnishments are legally mandated - must be processed exactly per court order.

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Request voided check for direct deposit verification
Double-check routing and account numbers
Verify all deduction amounts with source documents
Set clear effective dates for all changes
Scan and attach supporting documents (checks, W-4s, court orders)
Add memos documenting all financial setup and changes
Verify with member before making changes
Test direct deposit with small amount if available
Keep W-4 and enrollment forms on file
Review financial information annually with member

❌ DON'T:

Set up payments without proper authorization
Enter bank information without verification
Guess at deduction amounts
Skip effective dates
Forget to document changes
Change payment method without member request
Add deductions without enrollment forms
Process garnishments without court order
Skip verification step for account numbers
Common Questions

Q: What if member doesn't have bank account? A: Set payment method to "Check." Member will receive paper checks mailed to their address. Some organizations help members open bank accounts if check is not ideal.

Q: Can member have two direct deposit accounts? A: Depends on system capabilities. Some systems allow split direct deposit (portion to checking, portion to savings). Check your system features or contact administrator.

Q: What if member enters wrong account number? A: Payment will fail or go to wrong account. This is why verification is critical. If discovered before payroll posts, correct immediately. If after posting, may need to void and reissue.

Q: How long does direct deposit take to set up? A: Information can be entered immediately, but first direct deposit typically takes one pay cycle. Some organizations do pre-note verification adding additional time.

Q: Can member change bank accounts mid-month? A: Yes, but set effective date to next payroll to avoid complications. Changing after payroll runs but before deposit may cause payment failure.

Q: What if insurance premium increases? A: Update the deduction amount. For single member: Edit individually. For all members: Use
Mass Update Deduction Amounts
.

Q: Do I need member signature for direct deposit? A: Depends on your organization's policy. Many require signed direct deposit authorization form. Keep on file.

Q: What if member wants to stop all deductions? A: Edit each deduction and set amount to $0 or remove deduction entirely. Member must understand they're responsible for taxes if stopping tax withholding.

Q: Can I set up financial information before member retires? A: Depends on system and policy. Some organizations set up during retirement processing. Others wait until retirement effective date. Best practice: Set up before first payment due date.

Q: What happens if member doesn't set up financial information? A: They cannot receive pension payments through regular payroll. Payment will be held or member excluded from payroll until information is complete.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Routing number won't validate

Possible Causes:

Incorrect routing number entered
Transposed digits
Non-US bank (if system requires US routing)
Credit union routing number not in system

Solutions:

Verify routing number from member's check
Check all 9 digits carefully
Look up routing number on bank's website
Try hyphen variations if system allows (123-456-789 vs 123456789)
Contact bank to confirm routing number
Credit unions may have different routing for ACH vs checks
Issue: Cannot save - validation error

Possible Causes:

Required fields missing
Account numbers don't match
Invalid characters in fields
Deduction amount exceeds pension amount

Solutions:

Check all required fields marked with * are filled
Verify account number confirmation matches exactly
Remove special characters (spaces, dashes)
Ensure deduction amounts are reasonable
Check for error messages indicating specific issue
Issue: Member not appearing in payroll

Possible Causes:

Financial information incomplete
Payment method not selected
Member status not eligible for payment
Effective date is future date

Solutions:

Verify payment method is selected and complete
Check member status is "Retired" or eligible status
Verify all required financial information entered
Check effective dates - must be current or past for inclusion
Review locked payment accounts report
Issue: Direct deposit failed

Possible Causes:

Incorrect account number
Account closed
Account type wrong (checking vs savings)
Bank rejected transaction

Solutions:

Contact member immediately
Verify account is open and active
Confirm account number and routing number
Check with bank for rejection reason
Update information and reissue payment
May need to issue paper check for failed payment
