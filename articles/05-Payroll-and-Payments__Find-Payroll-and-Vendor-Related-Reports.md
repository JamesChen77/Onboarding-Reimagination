How Do I Find Payroll and Vendor-Related Reports?

Category: Payroll and Payments Time: 5-15 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

Payment Center access is enabled in your user profile
You have appropriate permissions to view reports
You know which report type you need (1099, Check Register, Vendor Register, etc.)
Your system has completed the required background jobs (if applicable)

Related Topics:

Run Retiree Payroll
Generate Payroll Comparison Report

What This Does

Guides you through accessing Payment Center to generate and retrieve payroll and vendor-related reports. This process walks you through initiating new reports, viewing available report types, and downloading completed outputs in your preferred format (PDF or XLSX).

Shows you how to locate, view, and download payroll and vendor payment reports in PensionPro+. Reports are stored in the Reports Center and can be filtered by category, date, and type.

Payment Center Is Your Reporting Hub:

Payment Center consolidates all payroll and vendor payment functions in one location, organized into cards for different functions including Reports, Vendor Management, Payment Processing, and Utilities.

Quick Steps
Navigate to Reports Center
Filter by "Retiree Payroll" category
Search by date or report name
Click report to view/download
Step-by-Step Instructions
Method 1: From Payroll Wizard (Fastest)

Where to Go:

Open Payroll & Vendor Payment Wizard in Payment Center
Navigate to Step 4: Create Reports
Scroll to bottom - Report Archives section
All payroll reports listed here

What You'll See:

Reports from current payroll session
Reports from recent payroll runs
Filtered to Retiree Payroll category automatically

Actions:

Click report name to open
Click download icon to save
Click delete icon to remove (if permitted)
Method 2: From Reports Center (All Reports)

Where to Go:

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Reports Center
Reports Center opens

Filter for Payroll Reports:

Category dropdown → Select "Retiree Payroll"
Or use Search box → Type report name or keyword
Click Search or Apply Filters

What You'll See:

All payroll and vendor reports
Organized by generation date (newest first)
Report name, category, date, status

Common Scenarios
Scenario: Find Last Month's Payroll Warrant

Steps:

Go to Reports Center
Category → "Retiree Payroll"
Look for "Warrant" in report names (reports are organized by date)
Find the warrant from last month
Click to open/download
Scenario: Locate Specific Member's Check

Steps:

Go to Reports Center
Category → "Retiree Payroll"
Search → Enter member name or ID
Find "Retiree Payroll Checks" report
Open and use Ctrl+F to find member

Steps:

Reports Center → Retiree Payroll category
Locate reports from the specific month (organized by date)
Download each needed report:
Checks
NODs
DD File
Warrants
Save to monthly payroll folder

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Filter by category to narrow results
Browse reports by generation date
Download important reports for backup
Organize reports in folders (by month/year)
Review reports before deleting

❌ DON'T:

Delete reports without review
Share unencrypted reports externally
Ignore report retention policies
Forget to download critical reports
Search Tips

By Date:

Reports are organized by generation date (newest first)
Scroll through the list to find reports from specific time periods

By Name:

Type partial report name
Use keywords: "warrant", "check", "1099"
Search is not case-sensitive

By Category:

"Retiree Payroll" for all payroll/vendor reports
Other categories mix with payroll

By Status:

Completed reports are downloadable
Processing reports still generating
Failed reports show error
Common Questions

Q: How long are reports kept? A: Typically 12-24 months online. Check with system administrator for retention policy. Download important reports for longer retention.

Q: Can I regenerate an old report? A: No, you generate new reports with current data. Historical reports reflect data at time generated.

Q: Why can't I find a report I just generated? A: Check batch queue - report may still be processing. Refresh Reports Center after 1-2 minutes.

Q: Can I schedule reports to generate automatically? A: Some reports support scheduled generation. Check with system administrator for availability.

Q: What if I accidentally deleted a report? A: Cannot recover deleted reports. You'll need to regenerate if data still available. This is why backups are important.

Q: Can I see who generated a report? A: Some systems show user info. Check report details or contact system administrator.

Q: Reports list is very long. How do I find what I need? A: Use filters! Category + Search dramatically narrows results. Reports are also organized by date, with newest first.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't find recent report

Solutions:

Refresh Reports Center page
Check batch queue for processing status
Verify you filtered correctly
Check if report failed to generate
Look in Report Archives in Payroll Wizard Step 4
Issue: Report won't download

Solutions:

Check browser popup blocker
Try different browser
Check browser download settings
Verify file permissions
Contact system administrator
Issue: Report opens but is blank

Solutions:

Report may have no data for period
Check filters used when generated
Verify payroll was posted for that period
Try regenerating report
