How Do I Manage Vendor Payments?

Category: Payroll and Payments Time: 15-20 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Before You Begin:

User must have Payment Center access permissions
Vendor information should be verified and complete (name, address, tax ID)
Payment method details available (bank info or mailing address)
Tax ID or 1099 reporting information if applicable
Know payment amounts and purposes
Understand payment schedule

What This Does

Use Payment Center > Manage Vendors to add new vendor profiles or update existing ones, including payment method, bank details, and printing preferences for downstream processing and reconciliation. This system allows you to maintain an organized database of all vendors your organization pays, ensuring accurate and timely payment processing.

Manages payments to vendors (insurance companies, legal firms, service providers) who receive payments related to retiree benefits or pension administration. Vendor payments are processed alongside retiree payroll in the unified Payroll Wizard.

Common Vendor Types:

Health insurance carriers
Life insurance companies
Dental/vision providers
Legal services
Tax authorities (if applicable)
Service providers

Vendor Management Features:

✅ Centralized Vendor Database

All vendor information in one searchable location
Quick access during payment processing
Multiple vendors viewable in grid format

✅ Flexible Payment Methods

Direct deposit (ACH) for electronic payments
Check payments for vendors without bank accounts
Payment method selection per vendor
Bank routing and account number storage

✅ Comprehensive Details

Vendor name and address
Contact person and phone number
Federal Tax ID and 1099 reporting codes
Payment preferences (check vs. electronic)

Common Questions

Q: How are vendor amounts calculated? A: Vendor payments typically equal the sum of member deductions for that vendor (e.g., all health insurance deductions go to the insurance carrier).

Q: Can I pay vendors separately from payroll? A: The system processes them together for efficiency. Contact your administrator if you need separate processing.

Q: How do I track vendor payment history? A: View Vendor Register (Step 6 of Payroll Wizard) or generate Vendor Payment reports from Reports Center.
