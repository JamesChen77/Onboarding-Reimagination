How Do I Generate Accounting Reports?

Category: Reports and Analytics Time: 5-10 minutes Difficulty: ⭐⭐ Intermediate

Related Topics:

Process Monthly Accounting
Generate General Ledger
Generate and View Reports Through Reports Center
What This Does

Accounting reports provide financial statements, general ledger details, trial balances, reconciliations, and other accounting-related information. These reports help you track financial activities, prepare month-end/year-end closing, and ensure accurate accounting records. You can access accounting reports from both the Accounting Center and the main Reports Center.

Quick Steps
Go to Accounting Center → Reports card
Browse the accounting reports category
Select the report you need
Enter required parameters (dates, accounts, etc.)
Click Generate Report
View or download the report
Two Ways to Access Accounting Reports
Method 1: From Accounting Center (Recommended)

Best for: Quick access to accounting reports while working in Accounting Center

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Accounting Center
Click the Reports card
The Reports tab opens with two sub-tabs:
Generate Reports - Create new accounting reports (Accounting category is pre-filtered)
Reports Archive - View previously generated accounting reports
The Generate Reports tab shows only accounting category reports
Method 2: From Reports Center

Best for: Accessing all report categories including accounting

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Reports Center
The Reports tab opens
Click Accounting category on the left
Accounting reports display on the right

💡 Tip: Both methods access the same reports - use whichever fits your workflow.

Common Accounting Reports
Financial Summary Reports

General Ledger Summary

Purpose: Overview of all GL accounts with balances
Parameters: As of date, account range
Use For: Month-end review, trial balance preparation

Trial Balance Report

Purpose: List of all accounts with debit and credit balances
Parameters: As of date
Use For: Verifying debits equal credits, month-end closing

Balance Sheet

Purpose: Assets, liabilities, and equity summary
Parameters: As of date
Use For: Financial statements, board reporting

Income Statement (P&L)

Purpose: Revenue and expenses for a period
Parameters: Start date, end date
Use For: Periodic financial performance analysis
Detailed Transaction Reports

General Ledger Detail

Purpose: All transactions for specific GL accounts
Parameters: Date range, account number(s)
Use For: Researching transactions, audit support

Cash Receipts Journal

Purpose: All cash received in a period
Parameters: Date range
Use For: Cash reconciliation, deposit verification

Cash Disbursements Journal

Purpose: All payments made in a period
Parameters: Date range
Use For: Payment reconciliation, expense tracking

Check Register

Purpose: List of checks issued
Parameters: Date range, check number range
Use For: Bank reconciliation, voided check tracking
Control and Reconciliation Reports

Control Accounts Report

Purpose: Compares control account balances to detail
Parameters: As of date
Use For: Ensuring subsidiary ledgers match control accounts

Bank Reconciliation Report

Purpose: Reconcile bank statements with GL
Parameters: Month/year, bank account
Use For: Monthly bank reconciliation process

Unreconciled Transactions

Purpose: Transactions not yet reconciled
Parameters: As of date, account
Use For: Finding discrepancies, reconciliation cleanup
Contribution and Payment Tracking

Contribution Summary by Account

Purpose: Contributions posted to GL accounts
Parameters: Date range, contribution type
Use For: Validating contribution postings

Payment Summary by Account

Purpose: Payments posted to GL accounts
Parameters: Date range, payment type
Use For: Validating payment postings

Pending Transactions Report

Purpose: Transactions awaiting posting
Parameters: As of date
Use For: Identifying unposted items before closing
Step-by-Step: Generate an Accounting Report
Step 1: Access Accounting Reports

From Accounting Center:

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Accounting Center
Click the Reports card
Generate Reports sub-tab opens (default)

From Reports Center:

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Reports Center
Click Accounting category
Step 2: Select Your Report

Browsing Reports:

Scroll through the available accounting reports
Each report shows:
Report Name - Clear description
Report Description - What the report includes
Click on the report name to open the generator

Search for Reports:

Use the search box at the top
Type keywords like "trial balance" or "cash receipts"
Results filter as you type
Click the report from search results
Step 3: Enter Report Parameters

Common Parameters:

Date Fields:

As Of Date - Report data through this date (for balance reports)
Start Date / End Date - Date range (for activity reports)
Use calendar icon to select dates

Account Filters:

Account Number - Specific GL account
Account Range - From account to account
All Accounts - Leave blank for all

Additional Options:

Include Zero Balances - Show accounts with $0 balance
Detail Level - Summary or detailed transactions
Sort Order - How to organize the report

💡 Tip: Required fields are marked with an asterisk (*).

Step 4: Select Output Format

Available Formats:

PDF - Recommended for:

Printing
Official records
Filing/archiving
Formatted viewing

Excel (XLSX) - Recommended for:

Further analysis
Creating charts
Manipulating data
Exporting to other systems

CSV - Recommended for:

Importing to accounting software
Database uploads
Custom data processing
Step 5: Generate the Report
Review all entered parameters
Verify date ranges and accounts
Select output format
Click Execute Report button
Progress indicator appears
Report generates and opens automatically

Processing Time:

Simple reports: 5-15 seconds
Detailed GL reports: 30-60 seconds
Year-end reports: 1-3 minutes
Step 6: Review the Generated Report

PDF Reports:

Report opens in viewer
Scroll through pages
Check totals and subtotals
Verify date ranges and filters

Excel Reports:

Data table displays
Columns show all report fields
Review for accuracy
Check formulas and calculations

Download Options:

Click format buttons at top to download
Can download same report in multiple formats
Step 7: Access Previously Generated Accounting Reports

From Accounting Center:

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Accounting Center
Click the Reports card
Click Reports Archive sub-tab
View all previously generated accounting reports
Reports are pre-filtered to accounting category

From Reports Center:

Go to Reports Center → Reports Archive tab
Select "Accounting" from Report Category filter
View all accounting reports

Downloading from Archive:

Find report in the table
Click format icon in Download column
Report downloads immediately
Common Accounting Report Scenarios
Scenario: Month-End Trial Balance

Steps:

Click hamburger menu (☰) → Accounting Center
Click the Reports card
Select Trial Balance Report
Enter As Of Date: Last day of the month
Select PDF format
Click Generate Report
Review that debits equal credits
Download for month-end file

Use: Verify all accounts balance before closing the month.

Scenario: Quarterly Financial Statements

Steps:

Generate Balance Sheet:
As Of Date: Last day of quarter
Format: PDF
Generate Income Statement:
Start Date: First day of quarter
End Date: Last day of quarter
Format: PDF
Download both reports
Combine for board presentation

Use: Provide complete financial picture for the quarter.

Scenario: Bank Reconciliation

Steps:

Generate Cash Receipts Journal:
Date range: Month being reconciled
Format: Excel
Generate Cash Disbursements Journal:
Same date range
Format: Excel
Generate Bank Reconciliation Report:
Month/Year: Current period
Format: PDF
Compare to bank statement
Identify reconciling items

Use: Complete monthly bank reconciliation process.

Scenario: Audit Support Documentation

Steps:

Generate General Ledger Detail:
Account: Specific account being audited
Date range: Audit period
Format: Excel
Generate Transaction Detail Reports:
Related accounts
Same period
Format: PDF
Organize reports by account
Provide to auditors

Use: Support external audit requests with detailed transaction listings.

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Run trial balance before month-end close
Generate reports immediately after closing periods
Use consistent date ranges for comparative reports
Download and archive year-end reports
Verify report parameters before generating
Use Excel format for analysis, PDF for official records
Generate reports during off-peak hours if very large

❌ DON'T:

Generate reports while transactions are being posted
Skip verifying date ranges
Assume reports auto-save (download important ones)
Generate very large reports during peak hours
Forget to check "As Of Date" vs date range reports
Mix fiscal year dates when creating comparative reports
Report Types Explained
As Of Date Reports (Point in Time)

Reports showing balances as of a specific date:

Trial Balance
Balance Sheet
Control Accounts
Account Balances

Example: Trial Balance as of 12/31/2024 shows balances on that date.

Date Range Reports (Activity)

Reports showing transactions during a period:

Income Statement
Cash Receipts Journal
Cash Disbursements Journal
General Ledger Detail

Example: Income Statement for 01/01/2024 to 12/31/2024 shows activity during the year.

Cumulative Reports (Year-to-Date)

Reports showing totals from year start through a date:

Year-to-Date Income Statement
YTD Cash Flow
YTD Transaction Summaries

Example: YTD Income Statement as of 09/30/2024 shows Jan-Sep activity.

Common Questions

Q: When should I run accounting reports? A: Run trial balances before closing periods, financial statements after closing, and detailed reports as needed for analysis or audits.

Q: What's the difference between summary and detail reports? A: Summary reports show totals by account. Detail reports list every transaction. Use summary for overviews, detail for research.

Q: Can I schedule accounting reports to run automatically? A: Currently, reports must be generated manually. Contact your administrator about automated scheduling needs.

Q: How do I export accounting data to external accounting software? A: Generate the report in CSV format and import the CSV file into your external system.

Q: Why don't my reports balance? A: Common causes: Unposted transactions, date range errors, or transactions posted after report generation. Run a fresh trial balance to verify.

Q: Can I customize accounting report formats? A: Standard reports have fixed formats. For custom layouts, use custom reports or export to Excel and format there.

Q: How far back can I generate reports? A: You can generate reports for any date in the system. Data availability depends on your system's history.

Q: What if my report shows unexpected data? A: Verify date parameters, check for unposted transactions, and review recent accounting adjustments that may affect balances.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Report shows zero balances or no data

Solutions:

Verify date range includes posted transactions
Check that transactions have been posted (not still pending)
Confirm account numbers are correct
Try running without account filters first
Verify you have permission to view the data
Issue: Trial balance doesn't balance

Solutions:

Check for unposted transactions
Run a fresh trial balance (regenerate)
Verify all journals are posted for the period
Check for transactions with invalid account codes
Contact your accounting manager if discrepancy persists
Issue: Report takes very long to generate

Solutions:

Narrow the date range
Limit account range instead of all accounts
Choose summary instead of detail
Run during off-peak hours
Contact administrator if consistently slow
Issue: Downloaded report format looks incorrect

Solutions:

Try a different format (Excel instead of PDF)
Check that software is up to date (Adobe Reader, Excel)
Verify download completed fully
Try generating again
Use a different browser
