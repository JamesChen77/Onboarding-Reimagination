What This Explains

Understanding where reports are located, how they're organized, and how to access them helps you work more efficiently. This guide explains the Reports Center structure, report categories, naming conventions, and the report lifecycle from generation to archiving.

Reports Center Structure
Main Navigation

Reports Center Location:

Hamburger menu (☰) → Reports Center icon (signal bars)
Section: Information (below Payment Center, above Message Center)
Always accessible from any page in the system
Two Main Tabs

Reports Tab (Generate New):

Create new reports
Browse report categories
Access custom reports
Search available reports

Reports Archive Tab (View Previous):

Access previously generated reports
Filter and search past reports
Download archived reports
Check report status

💡 Think of it as: Reports Tab = Library of available reports | Reports Archive = History of what you've run

Report Categories

Reports are organized into functional categories:

1. Member Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Member Reports category

Contains:

Member account statements
Service credit reports
Demographic reports
Beneficiary designations
Contact lists

Access From: Reports Center or individual member profiles

2. Financial Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Financial Reports category

Contains:

Contribution summaries
Balance reports
Transaction histories
Fund valuations
Interest calculations
3. Payment Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Payment Reports category

Contains:

Payroll registers
Check registers
Direct deposit reports
Payment summaries
Deduction reports

Also Access From: Payment Center → Reports tab

4. Accounting Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Accounting category

Contains:

General ledger reports
Trial balance
Financial statements
Cash journals
Control accounts

Also Access From: Accounting Center → Reports card

5. Year-End Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Year-End Reports category

Contains:

1099-R forms
W-2 reports
Annual statements
PERAC submissions
Year-end summaries
6. Compliance Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → Compliance category

Contains:

Regulatory submissions
PERAC annual reports
Audit reports
Valuation reports
7. Custom Reports

Location: Reports Center → Reports → View Custom Reports button

Contains:

User-created ad-hoc queries
Saved custom reports
Imported report configurations

Special: Not in categories - accessed via dedicated button

Report Lifecycle
1. Report Templates (Available Reports)

What: Pre-configured report definitions Where: Reports Center → Reports tab → Categories Status: Available to generate Action: Select to configure and generate

2. Report Generation (In Progress)

What: Report currently being created Where: Processing in background Status: Processing Time: Seconds to minutes depending on size Action: Wait for completion (system auto-refreshes)

3. Completed Reports (Archive)

What: Successfully generated reports Where: Reports Archive tab Status: Completed Available: Download in multiple formats Retention: Per system policy (typically 90 days to 1 year)

4. Failed Reports

What: Reports that encountered errors Where: Reports Archive tab Status: Failed Action: Review error, adjust parameters, regenerate

Report Storage and Access
Where Generated Reports Are Stored

System Storage:

Reports Archive (database)
Accessible through Reports Archive tab
Organized by date, category, creator
Searchable and filterable

Your Computer:

Downloaded reports go to your browser's Downloads folder
You control local storage and organization
System doesn't track downloaded copies
Who Can See Reports

Your Reports:

Reports you generate always visible to you
Listed in Reports Archive
Check "Show My Reports Only" to filter

Other Users' Reports:

Visible if you have appropriate permissions
Access controlled by system roles
Ask administrator for additional access

Shared Access:

Reports Archive is shared resource
All authorized users see same archive
Helps collaboration and prevents duplicate reports
Finding Reports Efficiently
By Time Period

Recent Reports:

Default view shows newest first
Sort by "Created On" descending
Today's reports appear at top

Historical Reports:

Use date range filters
"Created On Between" fields
Narrow to specific period
By Creator

Your Reports:

Check "Show My Reports Only"
Filters to your generated reports only

Specific User's Reports:

Search by name in "Created By" column
Click column header to sort alphabetically
By Category

Category Filter:

Select from Report Category dropdown
Narrows to specific type (Member, Accounting, etc.)
By Name

Search Function:

Type report name in search box
Filters as you type
Works on partial names
Report Naming Conventions
System-Generated Names

Format: [Report Type] - [Date/Period] - [Parameters]

Examples:

"Payroll Register - 01/15/2025"
"Member Account Statement - John Smith - 01/15/2025"
"Trial Balance - As of 12/31/2024"

Benefits:

Descriptive and self-documenting
Easy to identify in archive
Includes key parameters
Custom Report Names

You Define:

Name when creating custom reports
Should be descriptive
Include purpose or parameters

Best Practices:

"Active Members Contact List"
"Contributions Over $5000 - Q4"
"Members Missing Beneficiary"
Context-Specific Report Access
From Member Profiles

Location: Member Profile → Reports navigation card

Contains:

Member-specific reports only
Pre-filtered to current member
Quick access to common reports

Advantage: Member information auto-fills

From Accounting Center

Location: Accounting Center → Reports tab

Contains:

Accounting category reports only
Pre-filtered to accounting
Two sub-tabs: Generate and Archive

Advantage: Focused on accounting needs

From Payment Center

Location: Payment Center → Reports tab (if available)

Contains:

Payment and payroll reports
Pre-filtered to payment category

Advantage: Context-appropriate reports

Report Organization Tips
Archive Management

Keep Archive Manageable:

Don't regenerate reports unnecessarily
Check archive before creating duplicates
Use filters to find existing reports

Download Important Reports:

Save locally for long-term retention
System retention may be limited
Organize in local folders by year/type
Search Strategy

Efficient Searching:

Start broad (no filters)
Add category filter
Add date range
Use name search
Check "My Reports Only" if needed

Layer filters progressively to narrow results.

Batch Download Strategy

For Many Reports:

Filter to desired criteria
Download each needed report
Organize in local folders
Name files clearly if renaming
Quick Reference: Where to Find What
"I need to generate a new report"

→ Reports Center → Reports tab → Select category

"I generated a report earlier today"

→ Reports Center → Reports Archive tab → Check "My Reports Only"

"I need last month's payroll register"

→ Reports Archive → Date range filter → Search "payroll"

"I want to create a custom data export"

→ Reports → View Custom Reports → + Custom Report

"I need a member's account statement"

→ Member profile → Reports section → Member Account Statement

"I need accounting trial balance"

→ Accounting Center → Reports card → Trial Balance OR Reports Center → Accounting category → Trial Balance

Best Practices

✅ DO:

Check Reports Archive before generating duplicates
Use descriptive names for custom reports
Download important reports for long-term storage
Use category filters to narrow results
Understand report retention policies

❌ DON'T:

Generate the same report repeatedly
Assume reports are stored forever
Ignore failed reports (investigate and fix)
Clutter archive with unnecessary test reports
Forget to download critical reports
Common Questions

Q: How long are reports kept in the system? A: Retention varies by system configuration, typically 90 days to 1 year. Check with your administrator for your specific policy.

Q: Can I organize reports into folders? A: The system archive doesn't have folders. Use categories, filters, and local downloads for organization.

Q: What happens when a report is deleted from the archive? A: Per retention policy, older reports are automatically deleted. Download important reports to keep them.

Q: Can I restore a deleted report? A: No, deleted archive reports cannot be restored. Regenerate if needed (and data still exists).

Q: Where are downloaded reports saved? A: Your browser's default download folder (usually "Downloads"). Check browser settings to change location.

Q: Can I email a report from the archive? A: Download the report, then attach to email. The system doesn't have direct email-from-archive functionality.

Q: Why can't I see someone else's report? A: You may lack permissions. Uncheck "My Reports Only" and verify your role allows viewing other users' reports.

Q: Do reports take up storage space I need to manage? A: No, system administrators manage archive storage. You don't need to delete reports to free space.

Troubleshooting
Issue: Can't find a report I know was generated

Solutions:

Clear all filters
Expand date range
Uncheck "Show My Reports Only"
Click Refresh button
Verify report completed successfully
Issue: Too many reports in archive to find mine

Solutions:

Use "Show My Reports Only" checkbox
Apply date range filter (when generated)
Search by report name
Select specific category
Issue: Reports Archive is empty

Solutions:

Verify you've generated reports previously
Clear any active filters
Check permissions with administrator
Try refreshing the page
