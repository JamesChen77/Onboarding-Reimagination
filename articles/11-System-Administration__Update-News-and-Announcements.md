Update News and Announcements

Learn how to create, edit, and manage system-wide news articles and announcements that appear on the dashboard and news page.

Overview

News and announcements keep users informed about system updates, policy changes, important notices, and organizational news. Administrators can create rich news articles with categories, images, and publish dates that display prominently on the dashboard and dedicated news page.

Administrative Permission Required: Content management or administrator privileges

Understanding News Articles
What Are News Articles?

News articles are:

System-wide announcements visible to all users
Categorized content for easy organization
Rich text articles with formatting and images
Time-based content with publish dates
Dashboard widget content
Common Uses

System Announcements:

Software updates and new features
Scheduled maintenance notifications
System outages or issues
Performance improvements

Policy Updates:

Benefit policy changes
Procedure updates
Compliance notifications
Regulatory changes

Organizational News:

Board meeting notices
Staff changes
Important deadlines
Training opportunities

Member Communications:

COLAadjustments
Payment schedule changes
Open enrollment periods
Educational content
Access News Management
Step 1: Navigate to Content Management
Click user dropdown (top-right) → Settings (admin only)
Click Content Management
Select Recent News or News Articles
The News Article List displays
Step 2: View News List

The news list displays with:

Column	Description
Image	Thumbnail preview
Headline	Article title
Excerpt	Short summary
Categories	Assigned categories
Publish Date	Publication date
Active	Visibility status
Actions	Edit/Delete buttons

List Features:

Pagination (25 items per page)
Sort by publish date (newest first)
Search/filter functionality
Category filtering
Preview thumbnails
Create a News Article
Step 1: Start New Article
Click + Article button
The News Article modal opens
Modal displays empty form for new article
Step 2: Enter Headline and Excerpt

Headline (required)

Article title
Appears in dashboard widget
Shows in news list
Keep concise and descriptive
Example: "System Maintenance Scheduled for March 15"

Excerpt (required)

Short summary (1-2 sentences)
Appears in preview cards
Entices users to read more
Character limit: 200-300 characters
Example: "PensionPro will undergo scheduled maintenance on March 15 from 10 PM to 2 AM. All services will be unavailable during this time."
Step 3: Write Article Content

Article Content (required)

Full article text
Rich text editor with formatting options:
Bold, Italic, Underline
Headings (H1, H2, H3)
Bullet lists and numbered lists
Links
Alignment (left, center, right)
Text color and highlighting

Formatting Best Practices:

Use headings to structure content
Keep paragraphs short (3-5 sentences)
Use bullet points for lists
Include links to relevant resources
Bold important information
Use professional tone

Example Content:

System Maintenance Notification

Dear PensionPro Users,

We will be performing scheduled system maintenance on:

Date: March 15, 2026
Time: 10:00 PM - 2:00 AM EST
Duration: 4 hours

What to Expect:
• All system services will be unavailable
• No access to member records
• Payroll processing paused
• Reports cannot be generated

Action Required:
• Complete urgent tasks before 10 PM
• Download any needed reports in advance
• Plan accordingly for the downtime

We apologize for any inconvenience and appreciate your patience.

Step 4: Add Thumbnail Image (Optional)

Upload Thumbnail:

Click Upload Image or image placeholder
Photo upload modal opens
Click Choose File or drag-and-drop
Supported formats: JPG, PNG, GIF, WebP
Recommended size: 400x300 pixels (4:3 aspect ratio)
Maximum file size: 5MB
Click Upload to save

Image Guidelines:

Use high-quality, professional images
Avoid copyrighted images without permission
Consider stock photos or original graphics
Ensure image relates to article topic
Test appearance in both light and dark themes

Remove Image:

Click on current image
Click Remove in photo upload modal
Image clears, using default placeholder
Step 5: Assign Categories

Select Categories:

Click Categories dropdown or section
Select one or more categories:
System Updates
Policy Changes
Training
Announcements
Benefits Information
Technical Notices
General News
Other (customizable)
Multiple categories allowed
Categories help users filter news

Category Benefits:

Organized news browsing
Filtered views on news page
Grouped dashboard display
Better content discovery
Step 6: Set Publish Date

Publish Date (required)

Date article becomes visible
Default: Today's date
Can be future date (scheduled publishing)
Can be past date (backdated article)

Date Picker:

Click date field
Select date from calendar
Or type date in format: MM/DD/YYYY
Time component (if supported): HH:MM AM/PM

Publishing Behavior:

Future Date: Article hidden until publish date
Today or Past: Article immediately visible
Draft Mode: Can save without publishing (if supported)
Step 7: Set Active Status

Active Toggle:

ON (Active): Article visible to users
OFF (Inactive): Article hidden, can be published later

Use Cases:

Draft articles (inactive during editing)
Temporarily hide outdated content
Schedule for future activation
Archive old articles without deleting
Step 8: Save Article
Review all entered information:
✅ Headline is clear and concise
✅ Excerpt summarizes article
✅ Content is complete and formatted
✅ Image uploaded (if desired)
✅ Categories assigned
✅ Publish date is correct
✅ Active status is set
Click Save or Create button
Success message: "News article created successfully"
Article appears in news list
If active and published, appears on dashboard
Edit a News Article
Step 1: Locate Article
Find the article in the news list
Use search or filter to narrow results
Sort by publish date if needed
Step 2: Open for Editing
Click the Edit icon (pencil)
News Article modal opens with current content
All fields editable
Step 3: Make Changes

Update any of:

Headline
Excerpt
Article content
Thumbnail image
Categories
Publish date
Active status
Step 4: Save Changes
Click Save or Update button
Success message: "News article updated successfully"
Changes appear immediately on dashboard (if active)
Delete a News Article

⚠️ Warning: Deleting a news article is permanent and cannot be undone.

Step 1: Confirm Deletion
Find the article in the list
Click the Delete icon (trash)
Confirmation dialog appears:
"Are you sure you want to delete '[Headline]'?"
Click Delete to confirm
Or Cancel to abort
Step 2: Article Removed
Article permanently deleted
Removes from dashboard
Removes from news page
Cannot be recovered

Alternative to Deletion:

Consider setting to Inactive instead
Preserves article for potential reactivation
Maintains historical record
Safer than permanent deletion

Best Practices

✅ Do:

Write clear, concise headlines
Include all relevant information in article
Use professional images
Proofread before publishing
Assign appropriate categories
Set realistic publish dates
Archive old, irrelevant articles
Update outdated information
Test article display on mobile
Use consistent formatting

❌ Don't:

Use all caps in headlines
Create duplicate articles
Leave articles unassigned to categories
Use low-quality or copyrighted images
Publish without proofreading
Delete articles unnecessarily
Ignore outdated content
Overcrowd with too many categories
Use excessive formatting
Forget to set publish date
Article Formatting Tips
Headlines

Effective Headlines:

✅ "System Maintenance: March 15, 10 PM - 2 AM"
✅ "New COLA Increase Effective January 2026"
✅ "Updated Direct Deposit Procedures"

Poor Headlines:

❌ "Important!!!"
❌ "Read This Now"
❌ "Updates"
Excerpts

Good Excerpts:

Summarize key points
Include essential details
Create interest
1-2 sentences maximum

Example: "A 3.2% cost-of-living adjustment will be applied to all retirement benefits starting January 1, 2026. No action is required by members."

Content Structure

Recommended Format:

Opening paragraph - What, when, where
Details section - Supporting information
Action items - What users need to do
Contact information - Where to get help
Closing - Thank you or additional notes
Troubleshooting
Article Not Appearing on Dashboard

Check:

✅ Article is set to Active
✅ Publish Date is today or earlier
✅ Dashboard widget is enabled (user settings)
✅ User has refresh browser (Ctrl+F5)
✅ User is logged into correct tenant
Image Not Displaying

If thumbnail doesn't show:

Verify image uploaded successfully
Check file format (JPG, PNG, GIF)
Ensure file size under limit (5MB)
Try different image
Clear browser cache
Cannot Save Article

Common issues:

❌ Required fields missing (headline, excerpt, content)
❌ Invalid publish date format
❌ Network connection issue
❌ Insufficient permissions
❌ Content too long (character limits)
Formatting Lost

If rich text formatting doesn't save:

Use editor's formatting buttons (don't paste formatted text)
Avoid copying from Word (use plain text)
Check for unsupported HTML tags
Save frequently while editing
Test in preview before saving

Notes
News articles are tenant-aware (can be system-wide or tenant-specific depending on implementation)
Rich text editor supports standard HTML formatting
Images are stored in secure file storage
Article history may be logged in audit trail
Maximum article content length varies by system (typically 10,000+ characters)
Categories can be customized via application code values
News articles can be exported for backup (implementation dependent)
Dashboard widget shows most recent 3-5 articles by default
Users can dismiss or mark articles as read (implementation dependent)
Article view counts may be tracked (analytics feature)
Some systems support commenting on articles
RSS feed may be available for news articles
